# Grade 12 Mathematics curriculum research

Status: pilot baseline, 22 August 2026.

The Department of Basic Education's CAPS Mathematics Grades 10–12 document is
the authority for content. The applicable Annual Teaching Plan (ATP) controls a
particular year's pacing, and examination guidelines validate assessable scope.
Because ATP pacing can change, the API stores pedagogical `sequence` separately
from `term`.

Official source pages:

- https://www.education.gov.za/Curriculum/CurriculumAssessmentPolicyStatements(CAPS)/CAPSFET.aspx
- https://www.education.gov.za/Curriculum/AnnualTeachingPlans(ATPs).aspx
- https://www.education.gov.za/Curriculum/NationalSeniorCertificate(NSC)Examinations/ExaminationGuidelines.aspx

Proposed learner sequence: patterns/sequences/series; functions; finance;
calculus; probability; analytical geometry; trigonometry; Euclidean geometry;
statistics; integrated exam revision. This is a prerequisite-aware learning
sequence, not a claim that every annual or provincial ATP has identical weeks.

Before publication, a curriculum reviewer must record the exact ATP year,
revision, page/table, name and review date. Content is therefore labelled
`pilot`; incomplete topics are `outline`, never silently publishable.

## Worked-solution standard

Every example states its goal, reveals one learner-controlled step at a time,
preserves all revealed work (`display_mode: cumulative`), and pairs every line
of mathematics with a reason. Restrictions, units and rounding must be stated.
The final answer remains part of the full solution rather than replacing it.
