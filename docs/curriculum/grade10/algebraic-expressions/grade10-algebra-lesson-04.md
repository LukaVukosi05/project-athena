## g10-aex-l04-w01: Factorise x² + 2x − 3 fully.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_expressions", "values": {"lesson": 4, "model": "factor_groups", "a": 3, "b": 1, "c": 1, "sign": -1, "kind": "trinomial", "d": -1, "coefficients": {"2": 1, "1": 2, "0": -3}}}

1. Write the given trinomial x² + 2x − 3.

2. Find two integers with sum 2 and product -3: 3 and -1.

3. Split the middle term as 3x + (-1)x.

4. Group and take common factors to obtain (x + 3)(x − 1).

5. Answer: (x + 3)(x − 1). Expand to recover x² + 2x − 3.

**Answer:** (x + 3)(x − 1). **Misconception:** Preserve every sign, factor, restriction and original expression; check the final result by reversing the operation.

## g10-aex-l04-w02: Factorise x² + 4x + 2x + 8 by grouping in pairs.

2. Take x out of the first pair: x(x + 4).

5. Answer: (x + 4)(x + 2). Multiply back to recover the four original terms.
