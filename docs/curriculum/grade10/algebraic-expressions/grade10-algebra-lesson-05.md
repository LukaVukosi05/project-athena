## g10-aex-l05-w01: Factorise x³ − 8 completely.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_expressions", "values": {"lesson": 5, "model": "cube_identity", "a": 3, "b": 2, "c": 1, "sign": -1, "cube": 8, "kind": "difference", "middle": 2}}

1. Write 8 = 2³; identify a difference of cubes.

2. Use u³ − v³ = (u − v)(u² + uv + v²).

3. Substitute u = x, v = 2: (x − 2)(x² + 2x + 4).

4. Check the cross terms cancel after multiplying: the x² and x terms sum to zero.

5. Answer: (x − 2)(x² + 2x + 4). Multiply out to recover x³ − 8.

**Answer:** (x − 2)(x² + 2x + 4). **Misconception:** Preserve every sign, factor, restriction and original expression; check the final result by reversing the operation.
