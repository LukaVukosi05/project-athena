## g10-ag-l02-w01: Find the midpoint of A(-4; -1) and B(2; -3).

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_analytical", "values": {"lesson": 2, "model": "segment", "points": [[-4, -1], [2, -3]], "mode": "midpoint", "solution_point": [-1, -2], "answer": "M(-1; -2)"}}

1. Plot A(-4; -1) and B(2; -3).

2. The midpoint is halfway along the horizontal and vertical displacements.

3. Average x: (-4+2)/2=-1.

4. Average y: (-1+-3)/2=-2.

5. Answer: M(-1; -2). Check AM and MB have the same directed displacement.

**Answer:** M(-1; -2). **Misconception:** Keep the coordinates and axis scales visible; derive each conclusion from exact differences.
