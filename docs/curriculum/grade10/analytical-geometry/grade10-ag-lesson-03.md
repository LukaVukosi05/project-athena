## g10-ag-l03-w01: Find the gradient of the segment joining A(-4; -1) to B(-1; -2).

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_analytical", "values": {"lesson": 3, "model": "segment", "points": [[-4, -1], [-1, -2]], "mode": "gradient", "dx": 3, "dy": -1, "answer": "m = -1/3"}}

1. Plot A(-4; -1) and B(-1; -2).

2. Move horizontally from A by Δx=3; keep the x-axis scale fixed.

3. Move vertically by Δy=-1; down gives a negative rise.

4. Gradient m=Δy/Δx=-1/3=-1/3.

5. Answer: m = -1/3. Reverse both differences and the quotient is unchanged.

**Answer:** m = -1/3. **Misconception:** Keep the coordinates and axis scales visible; derive each conclusion from exact differences.
