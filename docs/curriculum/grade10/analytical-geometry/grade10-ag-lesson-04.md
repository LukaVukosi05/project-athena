## g10-ag-l04-w01: AB joins A(-4; -1) to B(-1; 0). CD joins C(-2; 6) to D(1; 7). Are AB and CD parallel or perpendicular? Justify with gradients.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_analytical", "values": {"lesson": 4, "model": "two_segments", "points": [[-4, -1], [-1, 0], [-2, 6], [1, 7]], "mode": "parallel", "answer": "AB ∥ CD"}}

1. Plot the four supplied points and draw the two segments.

2. Find mAB=(0−(-1))/(-1−(-4))=1/3.

3. Find mCD=(7−(6))/(1−(-2))=1/3.

4. The gradients are equal, so the segments are parallel.

5. Answer: AB ∥ CD. Compare the slopes, not the picture alone.

**Answer:** AB ∥ CD. **Misconception:** Keep the coordinates and axis scales visible; derive each conclusion from exact differences.
