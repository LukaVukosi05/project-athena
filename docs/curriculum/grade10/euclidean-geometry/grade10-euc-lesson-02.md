## g10-euc-l02-w01: In ΔABC and ΔDEF, AB=DE=6 cm, BC=EF=8 cm and AC=DF=10 cm. State the congruence criterion and the corresponding side to AC.

1. Draw both triangles with A↔D, B↔E, C↔F.

2. Mark AB=DE=6 and BC=EF=8.

3. Mark AC=DF=10: all three corresponding sides match.

4. Conclude ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF by SSS.

5. Answer: SSS; DF = 10 cm. Correspondence AC↔DF follows from A↔D and C↔F.

**Answer:** SSS; DF = 10 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.

## g10-euc-l02-w05: ΔABC and ΔDEF have ∠A=∠D=60°, AB=10, AC=7, DE=40, DF=28. Find the enlargement factor ABC→DEF and the similarity reason.

5. Answer: scale factor 4; SAS similarity. Correspondence A↔D, B↔E, C↔F.
