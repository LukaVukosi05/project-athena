## g10-euc-l03-w01: Kite ABCD has perpendicular diagonals AC=6 cm and BD=6 cm. Find its area.

1. Draw kite ABCD and its diagonals AC and BD.

2. Mark AC=6 cm, BD=6 cm and the right angle where they cross.

3. Use area=½×AC×BD=½×6×6.

4. Calculate 18 cm².

5. Answer: area = 18 cm². Both diagonal lengths were used once.

## g10-euc-l03-w02: In parallelogram ABCD, opposite side AB=7 cm and diagonals meet at O with AO=6 cm. Find DC and OC.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_euclidean", "values": {"lesson": 3, "a": 4, "b": 4, "c": 4, "model": "quadrilateral", "shape": "parallelogram", "operation": "diagonal", "base": 7, "height": 6, "answer": "DC = 7 cm; OC = 6 cm"}}

1. Mark both pairs of opposite sides parallel in ABCD.

2. Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal: DC=AB=7 cm.

3. Mark intersection O and AO=6 cm.

4. The diagonals bisect each other: OC=AO.

5. Answer: DC = 7 cm; OC = 6 cm. Do not claim that the two whole diagonals are equal.

**Answer:** DC = 7 cm; OC = 6 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.

## g10-euc-l03-w04: In kite ABCD, diagonal AC is the symmetry axis and BD=10 cm. The diagonals meet at O. Find BO.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_euclidean", "values": {"lesson": 3, "a": 6, "b": 5, "c": 5, "model": "quadrilateral", "shape": "kite", "diagonal_ac": 12, "diagonal_bd": 10, "answer": "BO = 5 cm"}}

1. Draw the kite with AC as its marked symmetry diagonal.

2. Mark BD=10 cm and intersection O.

3. The symmetry diagonal AC bisects BD: BO=OD.

4. BO=BD/2=10/2=5 cm.

5. Answer: BO = 5 cm. Check BO+OD=10 cm.

**Answer:** BO = 5 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.
