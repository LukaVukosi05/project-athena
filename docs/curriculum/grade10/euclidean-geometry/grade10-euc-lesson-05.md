## g10-euc-l05-w01: In ΔABC, D is the midpoint of AB and E of AC. BC=10 cm. Find DE and justify the result.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_euclidean", "values": {"lesson": 5, "a": 3, "b": 3, "c": 4, "model": "midpoint", "mode": "half", "ab": 6, "ac": 8, "bc": 10, "solution_de": 5, "answer": "DE = 5 cm"}}

1. Draw triangle ABC and mark AD=DB and AE=EC.

2. Label BC=10 cm.

3. Apply the midpoint theorem: DE∥BC and DE=½BC.

4. DE=½×10=5 cm.

5. Answer: DE = 5 cm. The short segment has half the corresponding base length.

**Answer:** DE = 5 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.

## g10-euc-l05-w02: In ΔABC, D and E are the midpoints of AB and AC. DE=5 cm. Find BC.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_euclidean", "values": {"lesson": 5, "a": 4, "b": 4, "c": 4, "model": "midpoint", "mode": "reverse", "ab": 6, "ac": 8, "de": 5, "solution_bc": 10, "answer": "BC = 10 cm"}}

1. Mark the equal halves on AB and AC, establishing both midpoints.

2. Label DE=5 cm.

3. The midpoint theorem gives DE=½BC.

4. BC=2×DE=2×5=10 cm.

5. Answer: BC = 10 cm. Check DE is half of BC.

**Answer:** BC = 10 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.

## g10-euc-l05-w03: In ΔABC, D and E are midpoints of AB and AC, and ∠ABC=50°. Find ∠ADE with a reason.
