## g10-euc-l06-w01: In ΔABC, D and E are midpoints of AB and AC. ∠ABC=40°. Find ∠ADE and give a complete reason.

1. Mark AD=DB and AE=EC in triangle ABC.

## g10-euc-l06-w02: ABCD is a parallelogram, diagonal AC is drawn, and ∠DAC=45°. Find ∠ACB and name the angle relationship.

1. Draw ABCD and mark AD∥BC.

3. ∠DAC and ∠ACB are alternate interior angles.

4. Alternate interior angles are equal on parallel lines.

5. Answer: ∠ACB = 45° (alternate interior, AD∥BC). The two angles lie on opposite sides of AC.

## g10-euc-l06-w03: In ΔABC, D and E are midpoints of AB and AC. BC=10 cm. A learner claims DE=10 cm. Find the first error and correct DE.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_euclidean", "values": {"lesson": 6, "a": 5, "b": 4, "c": 5, "model": "midpoint", "bc": 10, "de": 5, "mode": "diagnosis", "answer": "First error: DE=BC; correct DE=5 cm"}}

1. Draw the original triangle and mark AD=DB, AE=EC.

2. Mark BC=10 cm and the learner claim DE=10 cm.

3. The first error is treating the mid-segment as the full base: DE must be HALF of BC.

4. Replace the wrong step: DE=½×10=5 cm.

5. Answer: First error: DE=BC; correct DE=5 cm. Check 2DE=BC.

**Answer:** First error: DE=BC; correct DE=5 cm. **Misconception:** Use only marked geometric facts, state the theorem, and keep corresponding vertices in order.
