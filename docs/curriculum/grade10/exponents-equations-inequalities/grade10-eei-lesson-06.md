## g10-eei-l06-w01: A rectangle has perimeter 8 cm. Its length is 3 cm. Find its width w.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_equations", "values": {"lesson": 6, "kind": "word", "a": 3, "b": 1, "c": 2, "d": 2, "form": "rectangle", "perimeter": 8, "result": "w = 1 cm"}}

1. Use perimeter P=2(length+width): 8=2(3+w).

2. Divide by 2: 4=3+w.

3. Subtract 3: w=1.

4. Check width is positive and the units are centimetres.

5. Answer: w = 1 cm; 2(3+1)=8.

**Answer:** w = 1 cm. **Misconception:** Check the first sign, branch, equation, unit or domain restriction against the original question.

## g10-eei-l06-w02: Two adult tickets and one child ticket cost R14. An adult ticket costs R4 more than a child ticket. Find both prices.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_equations", "values": {"lesson": 6, "kind": "word", "a": 4, "b": 2, "c": 2, "d": 3, "form": "tickets", "total": 14, "result": "adult R6, child R2"}}

1. Let adult price A and child price C. Write 2A+C=14 and A−C=4.

2. From A−C=4, substitute A=C+4 into the first equation.

3. 2(C+4)+C=14, hence 3C=6.

4. C=2; then A=C+4=6.

5. Answer: adult R6, child R2. Check 2(6)+2=14.

**Answer:** adult R6, child R2. **Misconception:** Check the first sign, branch, equation, unit or domain restriction against the original question.
