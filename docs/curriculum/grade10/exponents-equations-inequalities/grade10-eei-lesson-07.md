## g10-eei-l07-w01: A trapezium has parallel sides 3 cm and 1 cm. Its area is A cm². Make the height h the subject of A = ½(3+1)h.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_equations", "values": {"lesson": 7, "kind": "literal", "a": 3, "b": 1, "c": 2, "d": 2, "form": "trapezium", "width": 4, "result": "h = 2A/4"}}

1. Start with A = ½(3+1)h.

2. Add the parallel sides: A = (4/2)h.

3. Multiply both sides by 2: 2A = 4h.

4. Divide both sides by 4: h = 2A/4.

5. Answer: h = 2A/4. Substituting into ½(4)h recovers A.

**Answer:** h = 2A/4. **Misconception:** Check the first sign, branch, equation, unit or domain restriction against the original question.

## g10-eei-l07-w02: In v = u + 4t, make t the subject.
