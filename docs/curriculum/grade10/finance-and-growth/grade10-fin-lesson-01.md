## g10-fin-l01-w01: R1,250.00 is invested at 5% simple interest per year for 36 months. Find the interest and final amount.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_finance", "values": {"lesson": 1, "mode": 1, "principal": "1250", "rate": "0.05", "years": "3", "periods": 36, "unit": "months", "interest": "187.50", "amount": "1437.50", "answer": "interest R187.50; amount R1,437.50"}}

1. Identify P=R1,250.00, i=5%=0.05, and t=36/12=3 years.

2. Use simple interest I=Pit because the same original principal earns interest each year.

3. I=R1,250.00 × 0.05 × 3=R187.50.

4. Add interest to principal: A=R1,250.00+R187.50=R1,437.50.

5. Answer: interest R187.50; amount R1,437.50. Check A=P(1+it)=R1,437.50.

**Answer:** interest R187.50; amount R1,437.50. **Misconception:** Choose the correct base, multiplier and period; keep the stated units until the last rounding.
