## g10-fin-l02-w01: A savings account starts with R1,250.00 at 5% compound interest per year for 3 years. Find its final amount and compare it with simple interest at the same rate.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_finance", "values": {"lesson": 2, "mode": 2, "principal": "1250", "rate": "0.05", "years": 3, "amount": "1447.03", "simple": "1437.50", "interest": "197.03", "answer": "compound R1,447.03; simple R1,437.50; difference R9.53"}}

1. Identify principal R1,250.00, annual rate 0.05, and 3 compounding periods.

2. Compound interest multiplies the updated balance each year: A=P(1+i)^n.

3. A=R1,250.00(1+0.05)^3=R1,447.03; compound interest is R197.03.

4. Simple growth on the original principal: R1,250.00(1+0.05×3)=R1,437.50.

5. Answer: compound R1,447.03; simple R1,437.50; difference R9.53. The extra growth comes from interest on earlier interest.

**Answer:** compound R1,447.03; simple R1,437.50; difference R9.53. **Misconception:** Choose the correct base, multiplier and period; keep the stated units until the last rounding.
