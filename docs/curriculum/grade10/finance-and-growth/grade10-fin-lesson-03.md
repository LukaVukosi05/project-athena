## g10-fin-l03-w01: An item valued at R1,250.00 rises with inflation by 5% each year for 3 years. Estimate its value after 3 years, rounding only at the end.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_finance", "values": {"lesson": 3, "mode": 3, "principal": "1250", "rate": "0.05", "years": 3, "amount": "1447.03", "direction": "up", "answer": "R1,447.03"}}

1. Identify the starting value R1,250.00, rate 5%, and 3 successive years.

2. Use one annual multiplier 1.05; each year acts on the previous value.

3. Write A=R1,250.00(1 ＋ 0.05)^3, without rounding the intermediate balance.

4. Calculate R1,250.00 × 1.05^3=R1,447.03.

5. Answer: R1,447.03. A multiplier below 1 gives a lower value; above 1 gives a higher value.

**Answer:** R1,447.03. **Misconception:** Choose the correct base, multiplier and period; keep the stated units until the last rounding.

## g10-fin-l03-w05: A town has 12,500 residents. Its population grows by 7% per year for 2 years. Estimate the population after 2 years, rounding once to a whole person.
