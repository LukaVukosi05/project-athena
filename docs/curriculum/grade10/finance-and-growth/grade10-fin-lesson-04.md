## g10-fin-l04-w01: A laptop costs R4,250.00 cash. A buyer pays a 20% deposit and finances the balance at 7% simple interest per year over 3 years, with no fees. Find the deposit, financed balance and monthly instalment.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_finance", "values": {"lesson": 4, "mode": 4, "principal": "4250", "rate": "0.07", "years": 3, "deposit": "850.00", "balance": "3400.00", "interest": "714.00", "amount": "4114.00", "months": 36, "instalment": "114.28", "answer": "deposit R850.00; balance R3,400.00; monthly R114.28"}}

1. Calculate deposit: 20% of R4,250.00=R850.00.

2. Financed balance is cash price minus deposit: R4,250.00−R850.00=R3,400.00.

3. Interest on the financed balance: R3,400.00×7%×3=R714.00.

4. The financed amount plus interest is R4,114.00 over 36 equal months.

5. Answer: deposit R850.00; balance R3,400.00; monthly R114.28. Divide R4,114.00 by 36; fees are excluded.

**Answer:** deposit R850.00; balance R3,400.00; monthly R114.28. **Misconception:** Choose the correct base, multiplier and period; keep the stated units until the last rounding.
