## g10-fin-l05-w03: One US dollar costs R18.50, then the rate rises to R19.25 per US dollar. How much more does a US$80.00 purchase cost in rand? Ignore fees.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_finance", "values": {"lesson": 5, "mode": 5, "principal": "80", "rate": "18.50", "years": 5, "new_rate": "19.25", "amount": "1540.00", "old_amount": "1480.00", "difference": "60.00", "direction": "compare", "unit": "ZAR", "answer": "R60.00 more"}}

1. The old and new quotes are both rand per US dollar: R18.50 and R19.25.

2. Hold the same foreign purchase of US$80.00 for both conversions.

3. Old rand cost = 80×18.50=R1,480.00.

4. New rand cost = 80×19.25=R1,540.00; subtract old from new.

5. Answer: R60.00 more. Check R1,540.00−R1,480.00=R60.00.

**Answer:** R60.00 more. **Misconception:** Choose the correct base, multiplier and period; keep the stated units until the last rounding.
