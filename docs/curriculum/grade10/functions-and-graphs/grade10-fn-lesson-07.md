## g10-fn-l07-w02: For y = -1·x − 2, find the average gradient from x=1 to x=2 using a secant.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_functions", "values": {"lesson": 7, "model": "function_graph", "a": -1, "q": -2, "base": 2, "given": [[1, -3], [2, -4]], "family": "linear", "mode": "secant", "answer": "average gradient = -1"}}

1. Plot the curve and mark x=1 and x=2.

2. Calculate f(1)=-3 and f(2)=-4; plot (1; -3) and (2; -4).

3. Draw the secant joining those two points; its horizontal change is 2−1=1.

4. The average gradient is (f(2)−f(1))/(2−1)=(-4−(-3))/1=-1.

5. Answer: average gradient = -1. This is one secant gradient, not the average of two instantaneous gradients.

**Answer:** average gradient = -1. **Misconception:** Keep domain restrictions, original points and the meaning of a and q visible through every graph step.
