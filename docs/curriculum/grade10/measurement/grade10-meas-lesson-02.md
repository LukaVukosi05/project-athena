## g10-meas-l02-w01: A right circular cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 5 cm. Its top is closed and its base is closed. Find its volume and exposed surface area in terms of π.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_measurement", "values": {"shape": "cylinder", "r": 3, "h": 5, "open_top": false, "volume": 45, "surface": 48, "lesson": 2, "answer": "volume 45π cm³; exposed surface area 48π cm²"}}

1. Label radius 3 cm and perpendicular height 5 cm; the base is a circle.

2. Base area = π×3²=9π cm²; volume = 9π×5=45π cm³.

3. Unwrap the curved wall: circumference 2π×3 multiplied by height 5 gives 30π cm².

4. Include the closed base (9π cm²) and the closed top (another 9π cm²).

5. Answer: volume 45π cm³; exposed surface area 48π cm². Surface counts only exposed faces.

**Answer:** volume 45π cm³; exposed surface area 48π cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.

Options: volume 432π cm³; exposed surface area 180π cm² | volume 432π cm³; exposed surface area 216π cm² | volume 864π cm³; exposed surface area 180π cm² | volume 432π cm³; exposed surface area 432π cm². Correct: volume 432π cm³; exposed surface area 180π cm². Label radius 6 cm and perpendicular height 12 cm; the base is a circle. Base area = π×6²=36π cm²; volume = 36π×12=432π cm³. Unwrap the curved wall: circumference 2π×6 multiplied by height 12 gives 144π cm². Include the closed base (36π cm²) but exclude the open top. Answer: volume 432π cm³; exposed surface area 180π cm². Surface counts only exposed faces.
