## g10-meas-l03-w01: A 4 cm × 4 cm × 5 cm rectangular prism is enlarged by multiplying each of its three dimensions by 3. Find its new dimensions, volume and total surface area.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_measurement", "values": {"shape": "prism", "a": 4, "b": 4, "h": 5, "scaled_a": 12, "scaled_b": 12, "scaled_h": 15, "scale": 3, "one_dimension": false, "volume": 2160, "surface": 1008, "lesson": 3, "answer": "12 cm × 12 cm × 15 cm; volume 2160 cm³; total surface area 1008 cm²"}}

1. Mark the original length 4, width 4 and height 5 cm; record the scale instruction precisely.

2. Change each of its three dimensions by factor 3: new dimensions 12 cm × 12 cm × 15 cm.

3. New volume = 12×12×15=2160 cm³ (original 80 cm³).

4. New total surface area = 2(144+180+180)=1008 cm² (original 112 cm²).

5. Answer: 12 cm × 12 cm × 15 cm; volume 2160 cm³; total surface area 1008 cm². All dimensions changed: volume scales by k³ and surface by k².

**Answer:** 12 cm × 12 cm × 15 cm; volume 2160 cm³; total surface area 1008 cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.
