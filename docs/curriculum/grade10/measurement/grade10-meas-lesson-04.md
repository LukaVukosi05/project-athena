## g10-meas-l04-w01: A solid sphere has radius 6 cm. Find its volume and total exposed surface area in terms of π.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_measurement", "values": {"shape": "sphere", "r": 6, "volume": 288, "surface": 144, "lesson": 4, "answer": "volume 288π cm³; total exposed surface area 144π cm²"}}

1. Mark the radius from centre to surface: 6 cm.

2. Choose the full-sphere volume formula: 4/3πr³.

3. Volume = 4/3π(6)³=288π cm³.

4. Sphere area = 4πr² = 144π cm²; it has no flat base.

5. Answer: volume 288π cm³; total exposed surface area 144π cm². Compare squared area units with cubed volume units.

**Answer:** volume 288π cm³; total exposed surface area 144π cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.
