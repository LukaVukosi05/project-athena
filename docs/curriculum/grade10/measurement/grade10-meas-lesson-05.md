## g10-meas-l05-w01: A closed right square pyramid has base side 8 cm and perpendicular height 3 cm. Find the slant height, volume and total surface area.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_measurement", "values": {"shape": "pyramid", "b": 8, "h": 3, "slant": 5, "volume": 64, "surface": 144, "lesson": 5, "answer": "slant height 5 cm; volume 64 cm³; total surface area 144 cm²"}}

1. Mark square base side 8 cm and perpendicular height 3 cm.

2. From the centre to midpoint of a base side is 4 cm; l²=3²+4²=25, hence l=5 cm.

3. Volume = 1/3×64×3=64 cm³.

4. Four triangular faces total 4(1/2×8×5)=80 cm²; add the base 64 cm² to get 144 cm².

5. Answer: slant height 5 cm; volume 64 cm³; total surface area 144 cm². Use the slant height for triangle area, perpendicular height for volume.

**Answer:** slant height 5 cm; volume 64 cm³; total surface area 144 cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.

## g10-meas-l05-w02: A closed right cone has radius 8 cm and perpendicular height 15 cm. Find its slant height, volume and total surface area in terms of π.

1. Mark radius 8 cm and perpendicular height 15 cm; the slant height runs down the side.

2. Use Pythagoras: l²=8²+15²=289, so l=17 cm.

3. Volume = 1/3πr²h = 1/3π×8²×15=320π cm³.

4. Curved area = πrl=136π cm²; add circular base 64π cm² to get 200π cm².

5. Answer: slant height 17 cm; volume 320π cm³; total surface area 200π cm². Height and slant height have different roles.

**Answer:** slant height 17 cm; volume 320π cm³; total surface area 200π cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.
