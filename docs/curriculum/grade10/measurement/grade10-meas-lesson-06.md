# Grade 10 Topic 10 · Lesson 6: Composite solids and first-error repair

## g10-meas-l06-w01: A solid is a right cylinder of radius 6 cm and height 5 cm capped by a hemisphere of the same radius. Its bottom is exposed and its joining circle is internal. Find total volume and outside surface area in terms of π. A learner claims volume 324π cm³; outside surface area 204π cm². Find the first error and give the correct measurements.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_measurement", "values": {"shape": "cylinder_hemisphere", "r": 6, "h": 5, "cylinder_volume": 180, "hemisphere_volume": 144, "volume": 324, "surface": 168, "lesson": 6, "answer": "first error: counted the internal joining circle as outside area; correct: volume 324π cm³; outside surface area 168π cm²"}}

1. Mark common radius 6 cm and cylinder height 5 cm; locate the internal join.

2. Inspect the learner’s claim: volume 324π cm³; outside surface area 204π cm². Mark the joining disc as internal.

3. First error: an internal circle of area 36π cm² was added to the outside surface.

4. Outside area is cylinder wall 60π plus exposed bottom disc 36π plus curved hemisphere 72π = 168π cm².

5. Answer: first error: counted the internal joining circle as outside area; correct: volume 324π cm³; outside surface area 168π cm². Count the cylinder wall, exposed bottom and curved hemisphere exactly once.

**Answer:** first error: counted the internal joining circle as outside area; correct: volume 324π cm³; outside surface area 168π cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish perpendicular height from slant, exposed faces from internal joins, and area units from volume units.
