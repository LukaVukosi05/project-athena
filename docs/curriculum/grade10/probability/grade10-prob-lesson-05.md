## g10-prob-l05-w01: In S of 8 outcomes, |A|=4, |B|=4 and |A∩B|=1. A learner claims P(A or B)=1 by adding P(A)+P(B). Find the first error and correct probability.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_probability", "values": {"lesson": 5, "model": "venn", "a_only": 3, "b_only": 3, "both": 1, "neither": 1, "total": 8, "a": 4, "b": 4, "union": 7, "answer": "Overlap counted twice; P(A or B)=7/8"}}

1. Draw the two events and put the shared 1 outcomes in their overlap.

2. Calculate the claimed numerator |A|+|B|=8; it includes the overlap twice.

3. Correct the first error by subtracting |A∩B|=1 once.

4. |A∪B|=4+4−1=7; divide by |S|=8.

5. Answer: Overlap counted twice; P(A or B)=7/8. The union count cannot exceed the sample space.

**Answer:** Overlap counted twice; P(A or B)=7/8. **Misconception:** The overlap is counted once in a union; complement requires both disjointness and exhaustive coverage.
