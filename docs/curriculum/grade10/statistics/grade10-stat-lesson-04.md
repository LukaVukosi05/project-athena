## g10-stat-l04-w01: Construct a box-and-whisker plot for [3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17], then give its five-number summary. Use the median-of-halves convention.

1. Keep the ten original values in ascending order: [3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17].

2. Median is the average of positions 5 and 6: (9+9)/2=9.

3. Lower half=[3, 5, 5, 7, 9]; its middle is Q1=5. Upper half=[9, 11, 13, 15, 17]; its middle is Q3=13.

4. Minimum=3, maximum=17; range=14; IQR=13−5=8. Draw whiskers and the box at those actual scale values.

5. Answer: five-number summary = (3, 5, 9, 13, 17). The box covers the middle 50% of the ordered observations.

**Answer:** (3, 5, 9, 13, 17). **Misconception:** Keep the original observations, axis scale and denominators visible; distinguish estimates from exact values.
