## g10-trig-l01-w01: In right triangle ABC, ∠B=90°, the side opposite ∠A is 3 cm and adjacent is 4 cm. Find sin A exactly.

1. Mark ∠B=90° and identify ∠A as the reference angle.

2. Label the side opposite A 3 cm and the adjacent side 4 cm.

3. Find the hypotenuse: √(3²+4²) = 5 cm.

4. Use sin A = opposite/hypotenuse = 3/5.

5. Answer: sin A = 3/5. Check 3²+4²=5².

**Answer:** sin A = 3/5. **Misconception:** Identify the marked reference angle, keep coordinate signs and test the original condition.

Use sin A = opposite/hypotenuse = 70/182. Answer: sin A = 5/13. Check 70²+168²=182².

- g10-trig-l01-r23: In right triangle ABC, ∠B=90°, the side opposite ∠A is 112 cm and adjacent is 210 cm. Find cos A exactly. Options: cos A = 8/17 | cos A = 17/15 | cos A = 8/15 | cos A = 15/17. Correct: cos A = 15/17. Mark ∠B=90° and identify ∠A as the reference angle. Label the side opposite A 112 cm and the adjacent side 210 cm. Find the hypotenuse: √(112²+210²) = 238 cm. Use cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse = 210/238. Answer: cos A = 15/17. Check 112²+210²=238².
