## g10-trig-l02-w01: Use the unit circle to find the exact value of sin 30° and identify its quadrant or axis.

1. Draw coordinate axes and turn counterclockwise from the positive x-axis to 30°.

2. Mark the terminal side in quadrant 1.

3. The reference angle is 30°; use its special-angle ratio.

4. Apply the sign of sin in quadrant 1: 1/2.

5. Answer: sin 30° = 1/2. Confirm the sign on the coordinate axes.
