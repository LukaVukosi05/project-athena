## g10-trig-l06-w01: Solve 1sin θ = 1/2 for 0° ≤ θ ≤ 90°; give the angle exactly.

Visual contract: {"family": "grade10_trigonometry", "values": {"lesson": 6, "family": "grade10_trigonometry", "opposite": 3, "adjacent": 4, "hypotenuse": 5, "orientation": "opposite-vertical", "model": "solve", "degree": 30, "fn": "sin", "multiplier": 1, "ratio": "1/2", "answer": "θ = 30°"}}

1. Identify the unknown acute or endpoint angle θ in 1sin θ = 1/2.

2. Divide both sides by 1: sin θ = 1/2.

3. Compare with the exact special-angle ratios.

4. The unique angle in 0° to 90° matching sin θ=1/2 is 30°.

5. Answer: θ = 30°. Substitute θ=30°: 1×1/2=1/2.

**Answer:** θ = 30°. **Misconception:** Identify the marked reference angle, keep coordinate signs and test the original condition.
