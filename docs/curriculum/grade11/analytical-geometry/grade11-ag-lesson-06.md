## g11-ag-l06-w01: A line through P(-3, -1) has gradient m = √3/3. Determine its inclination θ, 0° ≤ θ < 180°.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_analytical", "values": {"mode": "inclination", "p": [-3, -1], "theta": 30, "m": "√3/3"}}`

1. Plot P(-3, -1) and a horizontal positive x-direction through it.

2. Inclination θ is measured anticlockwise from the positive x-direction, with 0° ≤ θ < 180°.

3. Use m = tan θ = √3/3. The sign places the line in the acute inclination sector.

4. The reference angle is 30°, giving θ = 30° in the required interval.

5. Answer: θ = 30°. Check: tan(30°) = √3/3 and the plotted line has the matching direction.

Answer: θ = 30°. Check: Answer: θ = 30°. Check: tan(30°) = √3/3 and the plotted line has the matching direction.
