## g11-ei-l06-w01 — Determine the nature of the roots of x² + 6x + 6 = 0 without solving it.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_equations", "values": {"mode": "discriminant", "b": 6, "c": 6}}`

1. Read a = 1, b = 6, c = 6 from ax² + bx + c = 0.

2. Use the discriminant Δ = b² − 4ac.

3. Substitute: Δ = (6)² − 4(1)(6) = 12.

4. Because Δ is positive, the graph crosses the x-axis.

5. Answer: two distinct real roots. The sign of Δ = 12 confirms the classification.

Answer: two distinct real roots. Check: Answer: two distinct real roots. The sign of Δ = 12 confirms the classification.
