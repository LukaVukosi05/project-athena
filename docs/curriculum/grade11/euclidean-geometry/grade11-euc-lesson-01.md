## g11-euc-l01-w01: In a circle with centre O, OM ⟂ chord AB at M. If OA = 5 cm and OM = 4 cm, calculate AB.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "chord", "half": 3, "distance": 4, "radius": 5}}`

1. Mark OA = 5 cm as a radius. Because OM is perpendicular to chord AB, M bisects AB.

2. Triangle OMA is right-angled at M, with hypotenuse OA = 5 cm and side OM = 4 cm.

3. By Pythagoras, AM² = OA² − OM² = 25 − 16 = 9 cm².

4. The positive length AM = 3 cm, and the chord has equal halves AM and MB.

5. Answer: AB = 2(3) = 6 cm. Check: 3² + 4² = 5².

Answer: 6 cm. Check: Answer: AB = 2(3) = 6 cm. Check: 3² + 4² = 5².
