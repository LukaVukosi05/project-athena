## g11-euc-l02-w01: Arc AB subtends ∠AOB = 40° at centre O. C lies on the major arc AB. Find ∠ACB.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "centre", "central": 40, "target": "circumference"}}`

1. Trace arc AB and confirm that C is on the major arc; ∠AOB = 40° subtends the minor arc AB.

2. Draw chords CA and CB. The angle at C and the angle at O stand on the same minor arc AB.

3. The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on that arc.

4. Therefore 2∠ACB = 40°, so ∠ACB = 40° ÷ 2.

5. Answer: ∠ACB = 20°. Check: 2(20°) = 40°.

Answer: 20°. Check: Answer: ∠ACB = 20°. Check: 2(20°) = 40°.
