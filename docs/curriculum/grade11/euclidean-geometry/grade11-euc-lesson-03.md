## g11-euc-l03-w01: Points C and D lie on the same major arc AB. If ∠ACB = 22°, find ∠ADB.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "same_segment", "inscribed": 22}}`

1. Mark the given ∠ACB = 22° and identify its endpoints A and B.

2. ∠ACB and ∠ADB both stand on chord AB, with C and D on the same side of AB.

3. Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.

4. Transfer the 22° marking from C to D without changing the intercepted chord.

5. Answer: ∠ADB = 22°. The centre angle on the minor arc would be 44°, twice either angle.
