# Grade 11 Topic 4, Lesson 4: Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral

Scope: [DBE Grade 11 Mathematics ATP, Term 2](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2023%20ATPs/FET%20Content%20Subjects/Grade%2011/1.590%20ATP%202023-24%20Gr%2011%20Maths%20final.pdf). Original Athena diagrams and questions.

## g11-euc-l04-w01: ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral in order. If ∠BAD = 43°, find its opposite angle ∠BCD.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "cyclic", "given": 43}}`

1. Locate ∠BAD = 43° at vertex A and ∠BCD at the opposite vertex C.

2. Because all four vertices lie on one circle, the two opposite angles are supplementary.

3. Write ∠BAD + ∠BCD = 180°.

4. Subtract the known angle: ∠BCD = 180° − 43°.

5. Answer: ∠BCD = 137°. Check: 43° + 137° = 180°.

Answer: 137°. Check: Answer: ∠BCD = 137°. Check: 43° + 137° = 180°.
