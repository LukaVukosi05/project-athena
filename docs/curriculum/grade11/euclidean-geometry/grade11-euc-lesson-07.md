## g11-euc-l07-w01: Arc AB subtends ∠AOB = 40° at centre O. C is on the major arc AB and D on the minor arc AB. Find ∠ADB.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "centre_cyclic", "central": 40}}`

1. ∠AOB = 40° subtends the minor arc AB; C stands on the major arc.

2. The centre angle is twice the angle at C, so ∠ACB = 40° ÷ 2 = 20°.

3. A, C, B, D lie on one circle; ∠ACB and ∠ADB are opposite cyclic angles.

4. Opposite cyclic angles sum to 180°, so ∠ADB = 180° − 20°.

5. Answer: ∠ADB = 160°. Check: 20° + 160° = 180°.

Answer: 160°. Check: Answer: ∠ADB = 160°. Check: 20° + 160° = 180°.
