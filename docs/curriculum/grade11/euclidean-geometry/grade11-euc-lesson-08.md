Scope: [DBE Grade 11 Mathematics ATP, Term 2](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2023%20ATPs/FET%20Content%20Subjects/Grade%2011/1.590%20ATP%202023-24%20Gr%2011%20Maths%20final.pdf). Original Athena diagrams and questions.

## g11-euc-l08-w01: A tangent at A makes 32° with chord AB. C is on the major arc AB and D on the minor arc. A learner claims ∠ADB = 32°. Find ∠ADB and identify the missed theorem.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_euclidean", "values": {"mode": "tangent_cyclic", "given": 32}}`

1. Identify the marked tangent–chord angle at A as 32°.

2. By the alternate-segment theorem, ∠ACB = 32° because C lies on the major arc AB.

3. The learner transferred this value directly to D, but C and D are on opposite sides of chord AB.

4. ∠ACB and ∠ADB are opposite cyclic angles, so ∠ADB = 180° − 32°.

5. Answer: ∠ADB = 148°; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral. Check: 32° + 148° = 180°.

Answer: 148°; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral. Check: Answer: ∠ADB = 148°; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral. Check: 32° + 148° = 180°.
