## g11-es-l03-w01: Simplify √27 exactly.

Visual contract: `{"family": "grade11_exponents_surds", "values": {"family": "surd_pairs", "radicand": 27, "square": 9, "remainder": 3, "outside": 3, "lesson": 3}}`

1. Find a square factor of 27: 9 × 3.

2. Split the radical: √27 = √9 × √3.

3. The principal square root of 9 is 3, not a negative number.

4. Keep the nonsquare factor 3 inside the radical.

5. Answer: 3√3. Check by squaring: (3√3)² = 27.

**Answer:** 3√3. **Check:** Answer: 3√3. Check by squaring: (3√3)² = 27.
