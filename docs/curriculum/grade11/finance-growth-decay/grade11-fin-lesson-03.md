## g11-fin-l03-w01: An exchange board quotes R13.40 and later R13.05 for one EUR. Convert 87 EUR at the later quote into rand, and explain the change in rand cost.

Question-specific visual values: `{"family": "grade11_finance", "values": {"mode": "exchange", "direction": "foreign_to_rand", "currency": "EUR", "old": "13.40", "new": "13.05", "quantity": "87"}}`

1. The quote is rand per one EUR; use the later rate R13.05 per EUR.

2. For 87 EUR, multiply foreign units by rand per unit.

3. Calculate 87 × 13.05 = R1,135.35.

4. The earlier cost would be 87 × 13.40 = R1,165.80; compare the two.

5. Answer: Rand received = R1,135.35. A higher rand-per-EUR quote makes this foreign purchase cost more rand.
