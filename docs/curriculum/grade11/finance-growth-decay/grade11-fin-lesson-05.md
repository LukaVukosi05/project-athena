## g11-fin-l05-w01: R2,125.00 earns 7.00% nominal interest compounded 4 times per year. Find the effective annual rate to two decimal places, and identify the compounding period.

Question-specific visual values: `{"family": "grade11_finance", "values": {"mode": "effective", "principal": "2125", "rate": "0.07", "periods": 4, "years": 3}}`

1. Nominal annual rate j = 0.07; 4 compounding periods per year.

2. The rate per period is j/m = 0.07/4 = 0.017500.

3. Effective annual rate = (1 + j/m)^m − 1 = (1 + 0.07/4)^4 − 1.

4. This is 7.19% per year; after 3 years the principal would grow to R2,616.81 without intermediate rounding.

5. Answer: Effective annual rate = 7.19% effective annually. The period is 3 month(s), and the effective rate exceeds the positive nominal rate.

Answer: Effective annual rate = 7.19%. Check: Answer: Effective annual rate = 7.19% effective annually. The period is 3 month(s), and the effective rate exceeds the positive nominal rate.
