## g11-meas-l01-w01: A closed rectangular prism is 4 cm long, 3 cm wide and 5 cm high. Find its volume and total exposed surface area.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "prism", "l": 4, "w": 3, "h": 5}}`

1. Draw a rectangular prism; mark length 4 cm, width 3 cm and height 5 cm.

2. There are two faces of each type: 4×3, 4×5, and 3×5 square centimetres.

3. Volume = 4×3×5 = 60 cm³.

4. Exposed surface area = 2(4×3 + 4×5 + 3×5) = 94 cm².

5. Answer: V = 60 cm³; exposed SA = 94 cm². Check volume is cubic and six faces, including the base, were counted.

Answer: V = 60 cm³; exposed SA = 94 cm². Check: Answer: V = 60 cm³; exposed SA = 94 cm². Check volume is cubic and six faces, including the base, were counted.

## g11-meas-l01-w02: A closed right circular cylinder has radius 4 cm and height 6 cm. Give its volume and total exposed surface area in terms of π.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "cylinder", "r": 4, "h": 6}}`

1. Draw the cylinder and mark radius 4 cm on both circular ends and perpendicular height 6 cm.

2. The two closed circular ends contribute 2π(4)²; the curved side contributes 2π(4)(6).

3. Volume = π(4)²(6) = 96π cm³.

4. Exposed surface area = 2π(4)² + 2π(4)(6) = 80π cm².

5. Answer: V = 96π cm³; exposed SA = 80π cm². Check that both circular ends and the curved side were included.

Answer: V = 96π cm³; exposed SA = 80π cm². Check: Answer: V = 96π cm³; exposed SA = 80π cm². Check that both circular ends and the curved side were included.

## g11-meas-l01-w03: A closed right triangular prism has a right-triangle cross-section with perpendicular sides 3 cm and 4 cm, and hypotenuse 5 cm. Its length is 8 cm. Find its volume and total exposed surface area.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "triangular_prism", "a": 3, "b": 4, "hyp": 5, "length": 8}}`

1. Draw two congruent right-triangle ends with legs 3 cm, 4 cm, hypotenuse 5 cm; join corresponding vertices over length 8 cm.

2. Triangle area = (1/2)×3×4 = 6 cm². There are two triangular ends and three rectangular side faces.

3. Volume = triangular base area × prism length = 6×8 = 48 cm³.

4. Exposed surface area = 2(6) + (3 + 4 + 5)(8) = 108 cm².

5. Answer: V = 48 cm³; exposed SA = 108 cm². Check both triangular ends and all three rectangles are included.

Answer: V = 48 cm³; exposed SA = 108 cm². Check: Answer: V = 48 cm³; exposed SA = 108 cm². Check both triangular ends and all three rectangles are included.

Draw a rectangular prism; mark length 6 cm, width 3 cm and height 8 cm. There are two faces of each type: 6×3, 6×8, and 3×8 square centimetres. Volume = 6×3×8 = 144 cm³. Exposed surface area = 2(6×3 + 6×8 + 3×8) = 180 cm². Answer: V = 144 cm³; exposed SA = 180 cm². Check volume is cubic and six faces, including the base, were counted.

- g11-meas-l01-m02: A closed right circular cylinder has radius 6 cm and height 9 cm. Give its volume and total exposed surface area in terms of π. Options: V = 324π cm³; exposed SA = 180π cm² | V = 54π cm³; exposed SA = 180π cm² | V = 324π cm³; exposed SA = 144π cm² | V = 54π cm³; exposed SA = 144π cm². Correct: V = 324π cm³; exposed SA = 180π cm². Draw the cylinder and mark radius 6 cm on both circular ends and perpendicular height 9 cm. The two closed circular ends contribute 2π(6)²; the curved side contributes 2π(6)(9). Volume = π(6)²(9) = 324π cm³. Exposed surface area = 2π(6)² + 2π(6)(9) = 180π cm². Answer: V = 324π cm³; exposed SA = 180π cm². Check that both circular ends and the curved side were included.

- g11-meas-l01-m03: A closed right triangular prism has a right-triangle cross-section with perpendicular sides 3 cm and 4 cm, and hypotenuse 5 cm. Its length is 6 cm. Find its volume and total exposed surface area. Options: V = 6 cm³; exposed SA = 84 cm² | V = 36 cm³; exposed SA = 78 cm² | V = 6 cm³; exposed SA = 78 cm² | V = 36 cm³; exposed SA = 84 cm². Correct: V = 36 cm³; exposed SA = 84 cm². Draw two congruent right-triangle ends with legs 3 cm, 4 cm, hypotenuse 5 cm; join corresponding vertices over length 6 cm. Triangle area = (1/2)×3×4 = 6 cm². There are two triangular ends and three rectangular side faces. Volume = triangular base area × prism length = 6×6 = 36 cm³. Exposed surface area = 2(6) + (3 + 4 + 5)(6) = 84 cm². Answer: V = 36 cm³; exposed SA = 84 cm². Check both triangular ends and all three rectangles are included.
