## g11-meas-l02-w01: A rectangular prism measures 4 cm by 3 cm by 5 cm. Every dimension is multiplied by 3. Find its new volume and exposed surface area. State the volume and area scale factors.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "scale", "l": 4, "w": 3, "h": 5, "k": 3, "all_dimensions": true}}`

1. Draw and label the original prism: 4 cm by 3 cm by 5 cm. Mark the changed dimensions only.

2. Each dimension changes: 4→12, 3→9, 5→15 cm. The volume factor is 3³ = 27, the area factor is 3² = 9.

3. New volume = 12×9×15 = 1620 cm³.

4. New exposed surface area = 2(12×9 + 12×15 + 9×15) = 846 cm².

5. Answer: V = 1620 cm³; exposed SA = 846 cm². Check the volume factor is 27; the area changes according to the actual new faces.
