## g11-meas-l04-w01: A hemisphere of radius 3 cm is fixed to the top of a cylinder with the same radius and height 5 cm. The bottom circular base is exposed and the joined circle is hidden. Find the total volume and exposed surface area in terms of π.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "hemisphere_cylinder", "r": 3, "h": 5}}`

1. Draw a cylinder and hemispherical cap with radius 3 cm; label cylinder height 5 cm. Mark the joined circular face as hidden.

2. Total volume = cylinder πr²h plus hemisphere (2/3)πr³. Volumes add despite the hidden join.

3. Substitute V = π(3)²(5) + (2/3)π(3)³ = 63π cm³.

4. Exposed area includes cylinder curved side 2πrh, bottom πr² and hemispherical curved surface 2πr²: 57π cm².

5. Answer: V = 63π cm³; exposed SA = 57π cm². Verify the shared top circle is excluded exactly once from exposed area.

Answer: V = 63π cm³; exposed SA = 57π cm². Check: Answer: V = 63π cm³; exposed SA = 57π cm². Verify the shared top circle is excluded exactly once from exposed area.

## g11-meas-l04-w02: A closed-bottom cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 6 cm supports a right cone of the same radius and perpendicular height 3 cm. Their joined circular face is hidden. Find the total volume and exposed surface area in terms of π.

Question-specific visual givens: `{"family": "grade11_measurement", "values": {"mode": "cone_cylinder", "r": 4, "h": 6, "cone_h": 3}}`

1. Draw the stacked cylinder and cone: common radius 4 cm, cylinder height 6 cm, cone perpendicular height 3 cm.

2. The cone slant height is √(4² + 3²) = 5 cm. The common circular join is not exposed.

3. Volume = π(4)²(6) + (1/3)π(4)²(3) = 112π cm³.

4. Exposed area = cylinder curve 2π(4)(6) + bottom π(4)² + cone curve π(4)(5) = 84π cm².

5. Answer: V = 112π cm³; exposed SA = 84π cm². Check neither the cylinder top nor the cone base was added to exposed area.

Answer: V = 112π cm³; exposed SA = 84π cm². Check: Answer: V = 112π cm³; exposed SA = 84π cm². Check neither the cylinder top nor the cone base was added to exposed area.
