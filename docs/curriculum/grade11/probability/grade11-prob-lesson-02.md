## g11-prob-l02-w01: In a group of 22, 7 belong to A only, 3 to both A and B, 6 to B only and 6 to neither. Find P(not A).

3. Check 7 + 3 + 6 + 6 = 22.

4. The target not A contains 12 outcomes; do not double-count the overlap.

5. Answer: P(not A) = 6/11. Check the selected regions total 12 out of 22.
