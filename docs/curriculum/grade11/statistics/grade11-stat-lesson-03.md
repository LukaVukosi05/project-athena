3. Mark the midpoint of each bar top at [10, 20, 30, 40, 50].

4. Join the midpoint points to form the frequency polygon; the largest frequency is 13.

5. Answer: Modal interval = 25≤x<35, because its bar and polygon peak have frequency 13.

Draw a histogram and frequency polygon; identify the modal interval. Options: Modal interval = 15≤x<25 | Modal interval = 25≤x<35 | Modal interval = 5≤x<15 | Modal interval = 35≤x<45. Correct: Modal interval = 25≤x<35. The class boundaries [5, 15, 25, 35, 45, 55] define the horizontal positions; each frequency determines a bar height. Draw five touching histogram bars of width 10, heights [6, 7, 13, 9, 3]. Mark the midpoint of each bar top at [10, 20, 30, 40, 50]. Join the midpoint points to form the frequency polygon; the largest frequency is 13. Answer: Modal interval = 25≤x<35, because its bar and polygon peak have frequency 13.
