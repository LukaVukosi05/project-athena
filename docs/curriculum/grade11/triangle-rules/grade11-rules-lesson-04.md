## g11-rules-l04-w01: In ΔABC, a = 10 cm, b = 6 cm and c = 8 cm. Find angle A to two decimals.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_triangle_rules", "values": {"mode": "cosine_angle", "A": 90.0, "a": 10, "b": 6, "c": 8}}`

1. Plot the three sides, keeping a = 10 cm opposite unknown A.

2. Write a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A.

3. Rearrange cos A = (b² + c² − a²)/(2bc) = (36 + 64 − 100)/96 = 0.0000.

4. Use inverse cosine on the full-precision fraction: A = 90.00°.

5. Answer: A = 90.00°. Check substituting A into the cosine rule reproduces side a = 10 cm.
