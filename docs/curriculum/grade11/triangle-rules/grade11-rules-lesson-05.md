## g11-rules-l05-w01: In ΔABC, AB = c and AC = b enclose angle A. Draw altitude CD and derive the area rule.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_triangle_rules", "values": {"mode": "proof_area"}}`

1. Draw altitude CD = h perpendicular to base AB = c.

2. The triangle has area = ½ × base × height = ½ch.

3. In right triangle ACD, sin A = h/b, hence h = b sin A.

4. Substitute the height: area = ½c(b sin A).

5. Answer: Area = ½bc sin A. Check that A = 90° gives ½bc.

Answer: Area = ½bc sin A. Check: Answer: Area = ½bc sin A. Check that A = 90° gives ½bc.

## g11-rules-l05-w02: A triangular field has sides b = 8 m and c = 11 m enclosing angle A = 45°. Find its area to two decimals.

Visual values: `{"family": "grade11_triangle_rules", "values": {"mode": "area", "A": 45, "b": 8, "c": 11, "area": 31.112698372208087}}`

1. Draw triangle ABC and mark sides b = 8 m, c = 11 m and their included A = 45°.

2. Drop an altitude to base c; height h = b sin A = 8 sin(45°).

3. Use area = ½ × base × height = ½bc sin A.

4. Substitute: area = ½(8)(11) sin(45°) = 31.11 m².

5. Answer: Area = 31.11 m². Check the area is positive and no more than ½(8)(11) m².

Answer: Area = 31.11 m². Check: Answer: Area = 31.11 m². Check the area is positive and no more than ½(8)(11) m².

Options: Area = 32.00 m² | Area = 64.00 m² | Area = 55.43 m² | Area = 33.00 m². Correct: Area = 32.00 m². Draw triangle ABC and mark sides b = 16 m, c = 8 m and their included A = 30°. Drop an altitude to base c; height h = b sin A = 16 sin(30°). Use area = ½ × base × height = ½bc sin A. Substitute: area = ½(16)(8) sin(30°) = 32.00 m². Answer: Area = 32.00 m². Check the area is positive and no more than ½(16)(8) m².
