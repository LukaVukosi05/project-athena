## g11-tr-l01-w01: A right triangle has opposite side 3, adjacent side 4 and hypotenuse 5. Find tan θ from sin θ ÷ cos θ.

Whiteboard values: `{"family": "grade11_trigonometry", "values": {"mode": "triangle", "opposite": 3, "adjacent": 4, "hypotenuse": 5, "task": "tan"}}`

1. Use the given triangle to read sin θ = 3/5.

2. The same triangle gives cos θ = 4/5.

3. Because cos θ is nonzero, tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ.

4. Divide the fractions: (3/5) ÷ (4/5) = 3/4.

5. Answer: tan θ = 3/4. The opposite-to-adjacent ratio 3:4 confirms it.

Answer: 3/4. Verification: Answer: tan θ = 3/4. The opposite-to-adjacent ratio 3:4 confirms it.

## g11-tr-l01-w02: A right triangle has opposite side 5, adjacent side 12 and hypotenuse 13. Find cos θ and verify sin² θ + cos² θ = 1.

Whiteboard values: `{"family": "grade11_trigonometry", "values": {"mode": "triangle", "opposite": 5, "adjacent": 12, "hypotenuse": 13, "task": "cos"}}`

1. Mark the side adjacent to θ as 12 and the hypotenuse as 13.

2. The cosine ratio is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = 12/13.

3. The sine ratio is opposite ÷ hypotenuse = 5/13.

4. Square both ratios and add: 25/169 + 144/169 = 169/169.

5. Answer: cos θ = 12/13. Since 25 + 144 = 169, sin² θ + cos² θ = 1.

Answer: 12/13. Verification: Answer: cos θ = 12/13. Since 25 + 144 = 169, sin² θ + cos² θ = 1.

## g11-tr-l01-w03: A right triangle has opposite side 8, adjacent side 15 and hypotenuse 17. Evaluate sin² θ + cos² θ exactly.

Whiteboard values: `{"family": "grade11_trigonometry", "values": {"mode": "triangle", "opposite": 8, "adjacent": 15, "hypotenuse": 17, "task": "identity"}}`

1. Write sin θ = 8/17 from the opposite and hypotenuse.

2. Write cos θ = 15/17 from the adjacent and hypotenuse.

3. Square each ratio: sin² θ = 64/289, cos² θ = 225/289.

4. Add the numerators: (64 + 225)/289 = 289/289.

5. Answer: 1. Pythagoras gives 64 + 225 = 289.

Answer: 1. Verification: Answer: 1. Pythagoras gives 64 + 225 = 289.
