# Mastery assessment

Lesson 18 — Paper 1 mastery assessment

Duration: 75–90 min

Mastery objective: Demonstrate independent mastery across routine, complex and problem-solving questions.

Teach the idea

This lesson is assessment-led. Learners first work without hints, then use the whiteboard memo to compare reasoning line by line. A score alone is insufficient; error tags determine the required reteach lesson.

Assessment scoring guidance

Knowledge and direct recall: approximately 20%.

Routine procedure: approximately 35%.

Complex procedure: approximately 30%.

Problem solving and unfamiliar modelling: approximately 15%.

Record both total score and error tags. A learner may not advance on a serious CONVERGENCE or MODEL misconception without correction.
