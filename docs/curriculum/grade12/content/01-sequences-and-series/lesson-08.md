# Sigma notation

## Learning objective

Expand, interpret and evaluate finite sums written with Σ.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

Σ compresses repeated addition. The index starts at the lower limit and ends at the upper limit. Substitute each integer index into the general term. The number of terms from m to n inclusive is n−m+1.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Show Σ from k=1 to 4 of (2k+1).
- Name index, lower limit, upper limit and term.
- 2
- Substitute k=1,2,3,4 beneath the expression.
- The index changes; the rule stays fixed.
- 3
- Expand 3+5+7+9.
- Sigma means addition, not multiplication.
- 4
- Evaluate to 24.
- Keep expansion and evaluation separate.
- 5
- Reverse: write 5+8+11+…+32 using Σ.
- Find the nth-term rule and limits.

## Worked examples

- Question: Evaluate Σ(k=1 to 6)(3k−2).
- Worked answer: 1+4+7+10+13+16=51.
- Question: Write 4+7+10+…+40 in sigma notation.
- Worked answer: Σ(k=1 to 13)(3k+1).
- Misconception repair  Using only the upper limit in the term. Repair: limits tell which index values to use; the expression tells what to add.
