# Arithmetic-series applications

## Learning objective

Model totals where contributions change by a constant amount.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

First determine whether the question asks for one term or a cumulative total. Translate units and indexing, find any missing n, then apply the series formula. Interpret negative or non-integer solutions against the context.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Savings: month 1 R200, increasing by R50 monthly for 12 months.
- Identify a=200,d=50,n=12.
- 2
- Draw bars for monthly deposits, increasing linearly.
- Separate individual deposit from accumulated savings.
- 3
- Find T_12=750.
- This is the final monthly deposit.
- 4
- Find S_12=12/2(200+750)=5 700.
- This is the total saved.
- 5
- Label answers with rand and compare T_12 vs S_12.
- Different questions require different quantities.

## Worked examples

- Question: A theatre has 20 rows: 24 seats in row 1, +2 each row. Total seats?
- Worked answer: S_20=20/2[48+19(2)]=860.
- Question: A runner covers 2 km on day 1 and adds 0.5 km daily for 14 days. Total?
- Worked answer: S_14=14/2[4+13(0.5)]=73.5 km.
- Misconception repair  Adding a final term to a sum or reporting the last term as the total. Repair: label T_n and S_n before calculation.
