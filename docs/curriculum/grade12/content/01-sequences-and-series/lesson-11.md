# Deriving the finite geometric-series formula

## Learning objective

Understand and apply S_n=a(r^n−1)/(r−1), r≠1.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

Multiplying a geometric series by r shifts every term by one power. Subtracting the aligned expressions cancels the middle terms. Equivalent sign forms are valid; choose one and substitute carefully. If r=1, S_n=na.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Write S_n=a+ar+…+ar^(n−1).
- Show the finite geometric series.
- 2
- Multiply: rS_n=ar+ar²+…+ar^n.
- Shift terms by one power.
- 3
- Subtract S_n from rS_n; cancel matching middle terms.
- Only ar^n−a remains.
- 4
- (r−1)S_n=a(r^n−1).
- Factor before dividing.
- 5
- S_n=a(r^n−1)/(r−1), r≠1; note r=1 separately.
- State the restriction.

## Worked examples

- Question: Find 3+6+12+… for 10 terms.
- Worked answer: S_10=3(2^10−1)/(2−1)=3 069.
- Question: Find 160+80+40+… for 8 terms.
- Worked answer: S_8=160[1−(1/2)^8]/(1−1/2)=318.75.
- Misconception repair  Using r^(n−1) in the sum formula because it appears in T_n. Repair: subtraction leaves the next power, r^n.
