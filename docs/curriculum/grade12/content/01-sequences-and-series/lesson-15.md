# Mixed sequence and series decisions

## Learning objective

Choose the correct model and formula in unfamiliar questions.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

The difficult part is often model selection. Ask: one term or total? additive or multiplicative change? finite or infinite? known first/last term or known ratio? Write a short evidence statement before using any formula.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Display four prompts without formulas.
- Learner highlights words indicating term, total, percentage and forever.
- 2
- Build decision tree: T or S → arithmetic/geometric → finite/infinite.
- Model selection precedes substitution.
- 3
- Classify “increases by R80 monthly”.
- Constant addition → arithmetic.
- 4
- Classify “retains 92% yearly”.
- Constant multiplier → geometric.
- 5
- Classify “total after 12 months” vs “amount in month 12”.
- S_12 and T_12 answer different questions.

## Worked examples

- Question: Deposit R300 then +R40 monthly. Total after 10 months?
- Worked answer: Arithmetic series: S_10=4 800.
- Question: Machine output begins 500 units and falls 8% each cycle. Output in cycle 9?
- Worked answer: Geometric term: 500(0.92)^8≈256.61.
- Misconception repair  Searching the formula sheet for matching symbols before understanding the situation. Repair: write the change type and target quantity in words first.
