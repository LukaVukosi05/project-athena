# Algebraic and integrated exam problems

## Learning objective

Solve multi-step problems combining terms, sums, equations and constraints.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

## Full concept explanation

Integrated questions require a chain: translate conditions, solve parameters, construct a rule, then calculate or prove the requested result. Keep exact values until the final line and test all candidate solutions against sequence restrictions.

## Worked examples

- Question: Arithmetic T_4=14,S_10=185. Find T_25.
- Worked answer: a=5,d=3; T_25=77.
- Question: Geometric positive terms: T_2=6 and S_3=42. Find a,r.
- Worked answer: ar=6 and a(1+r+r²)=42. With a=6/r: 1/r+1+r=7; r=(6±4√2)/2=3±2√2, with corresponding a=6/r; both positive candidates satisfy unless further restriction is given.
- Misconception repair  Discarding a mathematically valid second solution without a stated condition. Repair: apply only actual restrictions such as positive ratio, integer terms or 0<r<1.
