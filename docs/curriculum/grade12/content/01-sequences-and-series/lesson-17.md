# Error clinic and proof of understanding

## Learning objective

Diagnose, explain and repair common Grade 12 sequence errors.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

Error analysis develops stronger transfer than repeating correct procedures. A complete correction names the first wrong step, explains the violated idea and supplies a valid replacement.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Incorrect: T_10=5+10(3).
- Highlight the first wrong symbol: 10 instead of 9 jumps.
- 2
- Repair: T_10=5+(10−1)3=32.
- Connect correction to term positions.
- 3
- Incorrect: S∞ of 4+8+16+… =4/(1−2)=−4.
- The convergence condition failed before calculation.
- 4
- Incorrect: second difference 6, so a=6.
- Repair using 2a=6, therefore a=3.
- 5
- Learner writes a one-sentence principle for each repair.
- Generalise beyond the single question.

## Worked examples

- Question: Correct “r=next−previous” for 3,12,48,…
- Worked answer: r=next/previous=4.
- Question: Correct “0.777…=7/10”.
- Worked answer: It is an infinite sum: (7/10)/(1−1/10)=7/9.
- Misconception repair  Treating a final correct answer as proof that the method is sound. Repair: marks are awarded for valid reasoning; accidental answers are not mastery.
