# Paper 1 mastery assessment

## Learning objective

Demonstrate independent mastery across routine, complex and problem-solving questions.

## Prerequisite diagnostic

Check the prerequisite concepts listed in the source teaching sequence before beginning the worked examples.

## Full concept explanation

This lesson is assessment-led. Learners first work without hints, then use the whiteboard memo to compare reasoning line by line. A score alone is insufficient; error tags determine the required reteach lesson.

## Vocabulary and notation

Use the notation and mathematical vocabulary introduced in the concept explanation exactly as written.

## Whiteboard teaching sequence

- Frame
- Board state / animation
- Teacher narration or prompt
- 1
- Display assessment map: patterns, terms, finite sums, infinite sums, applications.
- Set expectations and calculator rules.
- 2
- After submission, reveal one line at a time, not the final answer.
- Learners compare the first divergence from the memo.
- 3
- Colour-code marks for formula, substitution, simplification and interpretation.
- Show where method marks are earned.
- 4
- Attach error tags: INDEX, MODEL, RATIO, CONVERGENCE, ALGEBRA.
- Errors route to specific lessons.
- 5
- Generate mastery outcome: ≥80% advance; 60–79% targeted practice; <60% reteach.
- Use evidence to choose the next action.

## Worked examples

- Question: Assessment Q1: Find the 50th term of 11,18,25,… (3)
- Worked answer: T_50=11+49(7)=354.
- Question: Assessment Q2: Find the sum 6+12+24+…+6(2^9). (4)
- Worked answer: 10 terms; S_10=6(2^10−1)=6 138.
- Misconception repair  Reviewing only questions answered incorrectly. Repair: also inspect guessed or inefficient correct solutions because they may hide fragile understanding.
