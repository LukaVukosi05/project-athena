# Topic 2 scope and sources

## Authority and provenance

This authoring pack uses the required authority order. It is not wired into the application.

1. **DBE CAPS Mathematics Grades 10–12** — primary curriculum authority. The Grade 12 Paper 1 table assigns *Algebra and equations (and inequalities)* **25 ± 3 marks**. CAPS also prescribes the approximate cognitive distribution: knowledge 20%, routine procedures 35%, complex procedures 30%, problem-solving 15%. Source: <https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/CD/National%20Curriculum%20Statements%20and%20Vocational/CAPS%20FET%20_%20MATHEMATICS%20_%20GR%2010-12%20_%20Web_1133.pdf> (especially pp. 17–18, 54–57, 59).
2. **DBE Grade 12 Mathematics Examination Guidelines** — depth and examination boundary authority. DBE states that the guidelines supplement CAPS. Source page: <https://www.education.gov.za/2021Grade12ExamGuidelines/tabid/2720/ItemId/25878/Default.aspx>.
3. **DBE Grade 12 Mathematics revision/examination support manual** — confirms that completing the square is not examined as a solving method; substitution/k-method and surd equations leading to quadratics are examinable; non-quadratic inequalities connect to functions; nature of roots connects to quadratics and prescribed functions. Source: <https://www.education.gov.za/LinkClick.aspx?fileticket=7u1qDeDry9M%3D&mid=6780&portalid=0&tabid=1706> (p. 3).
4. **DBE state-owned Grade 12 Mathematics learner and teacher guides** — consulted as teaching-support provenance, not as authority over CAPS. Catalogue: <https://www.education.gov.za/Curriculum/LearningandTeachingSupportMaterials(LTSM)/DigitalContent/StateOwnedTextbooksGrade10to12.aspx>.
5. **Topic 1 authoring reference** — `Project_Athena_Grade12_Topic1_Sequences_and_Series_Lesson_Pack.docx` was inspected as the local standard for lesson structure, teacher voice, semantic whiteboard specifications, practice support, mastery and QA. It is not a mathematics authority for Topic 2 and was not modified.

### Missing authority

No current official Grade 12 Mathematics Annual Teaching Plan was present in the repository, and a current official ATP specific to Grade 12 Mathematics could not be verified on the DBE site during this authoring pass. Consequently, the manifest does **not** assert a term/week allocation. That metadata remains `pending_current_official_document` and must be reviewed before publication.

No past-paper question has been copied. Examination style is represented through original questions that assess the same skills.

## Authoring decisions

- Lesson 1 diagnoses prerequisite gaps without reteaching a full Grade 10 course.
- Factorisation is always checked by expansion.
- A denominator restriction is recorded before simplifying and survives cancellation.
- Only identical, non-zero **factors** cancel; terms joined by addition or subtraction never cancel.
- Equation balance is represented through the same legal operation on both sides, never “move and change sign”.
- Practice solutions remain unavailable until an attempt/support decision in the eventual runtime; this pack specifies content only.
- Semantic whiteboard events are data labels, never executable JavaScript.
