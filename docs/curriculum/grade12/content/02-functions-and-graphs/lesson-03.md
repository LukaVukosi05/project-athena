## Mastery objective

Analyse y=mx+c and connect gradient to constant rate of change.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Mastery objective: Analyse y=mx+c and connect gradient to constant rate of change.

Stable lesson ID: g12-functions-03

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

For a non-vertical line, m is the change in y per unit change in x and c is the y-intercept. Parallel lines have equal gradients; perpendicular non-vertical lines have gradients whose product is −1.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw a slope triangle between two exact points. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 2 | Calculate m=(y2−y1)/(x2−x1), preserving order. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 3 | Move c while keeping m fixed to show vertical translation. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 4 | Change the sign and magnitude of m. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 5 | Construct an equation from a point and gradient. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |

## Worked examples

Question: Line through (2,5) and (6,13).

Worked answer: m=2; y=2x+1.

Question: Line perpendicular to y=−3x+4 through (3,2).

Worked answer: m=1/3; y−2=(1/3)(x−3), so y=x/3+1.

Misconception repair  Using reciprocal instead of negative reciprocal for perpendicular lines. Repair: verify m1m2=−1.
