## Mastery objective

Analyse y=a/(x−p)+q, its asymptotes, branches, intercepts and transformations.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Mastery objective: Analyse y=a/(x−p)+q, its asymptotes, branches, intercepts and transformations.

Stable lesson ID: g12-functions-06

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

The vertical asymptote x=p is excluded from the domain, and horizontal asymptote y=q is excluded from the range. The sign of a controls branch orientation relative to the asymptote intersection.

| 5 | Find intercepts algebraically and verify on graph. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |

## Worked examples

Question: Features of y=−6/(x+2)+3.

Worked answer: Asymptotes x=−2,y=3; domain x≠−2; range y≠3.

Question: Find intercepts of y=4/(x−1)−2.

Worked answer: x-intercept (3,0); y-intercept (0,−6).

Misconception repair  Treating p and q as intercepts. Repair: asymptotes are reference lines; intercepts require x=0 or y=0.
