## Teach the idea

A logarithm answers: to what exponent must the base be raised? y=log_b(x) is the inverse of y=b^x, so the graphs reflect in y=x. The logarithm requires x>0, b>0 and b≠1.

## Worked examples

Question: Solve log_3(x)=4.

Worked answer: x=3^4=81.

Question: Solve 5^(x−1)=125.

Worked answer: 125=5³, so x−1=3 and x=4.

Misconception repair  Treating log_b(x) as b/x. Repair: rewrite every logarithm as an equivalent exponential statement.
