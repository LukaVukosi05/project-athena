## Mastery objective

Find and graph the inverse relation of a restricted parabola.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Mastery objective: Find and graph the inverse relation of a restricted parabola.

Stable lesson ID: g12-functions-10

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A full parabola fails the horizontal-line test. After restricting to one side of the axis of symmetry, swapping x and y produces a square-root function. The original range becomes the inverse domain.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Show y=(x−2)²−1 and axis x=2. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 2 | Restrict original domain to x≥2. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 3 | Swap x,y: x=(y−2)²−1. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 4 | Solve y=2+sqrt(x+1), selecting branch from restriction. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 5 | Overlay reflection and match domain/range. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |

## Worked examples

Question: Inverse of y=(x+1)², x≥−1.

Worked answer: f^−1(x)=−1+sqrt(x), x≥0.

Question: Original y=−(x−3)²+4, x≥3.

Worked answer: Inverse y=3+sqrt(4−x), domain x≤4.

Misconception repair  Keeping both ± branches after the original domain was restricted. Repair: the chosen side of the parabola determines the inverse branch.
