## Mastery objective

Use graphs to solve equations and interval inequalities.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Mastery objective: Use graphs to solve equations and interval inequalities.

Stable lesson ID: g12-functions-13

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

Roots solve f(x)=0; intersections solve f(x)=g(x). For inequalities, identify boundary x-values and test/read the intervals where one graph lies above or below the other.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Plot f(x)=x²−5x+6 and mark roots 2,3. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 2 | Shade where f(x)<0 between the roots. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 3 | Shade where f(x)>0 outside the roots. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 4 | Compare f and line g using vertical position. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 5 | Write solutions with correct open/closed endpoints. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |

## Worked examples

Question: Solve x²−5x+6≤0.

Worked answer: 2≤x≤3.

Question: Solve graphically 2^x>4.

Worked answer: x>2.

Misconception repair  Writing y-values as the answer to an inequality in x. Repair: project valid graph regions onto the x-axis.
