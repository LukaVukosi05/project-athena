## Mastery objective

Calculate and interpret average rate of change on a function.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Mastery objective: Calculate and interpret average rate of change on a function.

Stable lesson ID: g12-functions-14

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

Average gradient between x=a and x=b is [f(b)−f(a)]/(b−a). It is the slope of the secant line and describes average output change per unit input change.

## Worked examples

Question: Average gradient of x²−2x from x=1 to 5.

Worked answer: [15−(−1)]/4=4.

Question: Interpret −120 rand/year.

Worked answer: The value decreases on average by R120 per year.

Misconception repair  Dividing f(b)−f(a) by b−a inconsistently ordered. Repair: use the same endpoint order in numerator and denominator.
