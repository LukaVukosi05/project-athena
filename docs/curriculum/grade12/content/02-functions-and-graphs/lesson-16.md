## Teach the idea

Integrated questions may combine intercepts, transformations, inverse functions, inequalities and parameter solving. Treat each result as information for the next part and preserve exact values.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Display f(x)=−(x−2)²+9 and line g(x)=x+3. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 2 | Read vertex and range of f. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 3 | Solve f=g for intersections. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 4 | Use graph position to solve f(x)>g(x). | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |
| 5 | Restrict f to x≥2 and sketch inverse relation. | Explain, predict, calculate or verify this change before continuing. |

## Worked examples

Question: For f=−(x−2)²+9 and g=x+3, intersections.

Worked answer: x²−3x−2=0, so x=(3±sqrt17)/2; y=x+3.

Question: State f range and inverse restriction.

Worked answer: Range y≤9; restrict x≥2 or x≤2.

Misconception repair  Rounding intersection values early and corrupting later intervals. Repair: keep surds/exact expressions until final presentation.
