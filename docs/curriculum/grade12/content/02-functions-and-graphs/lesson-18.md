## Worked examples

Question: Analyse y=−2(x−3)²+8.

Worked answer: Vertex (3,8), axis x=3, range y≤8, roots 1 and 5.

Question: Find inverse of y=3x−7.

Worked answer: f^−1(x)=(x+7)/3.

Misconception repair  Treating a correct multiple-choice selection as mastery without reasoning. Repair: require working on high-value items.
