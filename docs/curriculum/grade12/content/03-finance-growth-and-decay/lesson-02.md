## Mastery objective

Calculate compound growth with rates and time expressed in compatible periods.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-02

Mastery objective: Calculate compound growth with rates and time expressed in compatible periods.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A quoted annual rate is not automatically the rate used in the exponent. If interest is compounded m times per year, the periodic rate is the nominal annual rate divided by m and the number of periods is years multiplied by m.

Formula and notation anchor  A=P(1+j/m)^(mt), where j is a nominal annual rate compounded m times per year.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw a 3-year monthly timeline and count 36 periods. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Highlight j=8.4% p.a. compounded monthly. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Reveal i=0.084/12 and n=36 together. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Animate the balance multiplying by (1+i) each month. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify the final balance exceeds P and round money only at the end. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Invest R12 500 for 3 years at 8.4% p.a. compounded monthly.

Worked method: A=12 500(1+0.084/12)^36≈R16 068.34.

Question: R20 000 grows to R27 000 at 7.2% p.a. compounded quarterly. Set up an equation for time.

Worked method: 27 000=20 000(1+0.072/4)^(4t); solve t with logarithms if required.

Misconception repair  Using i=0.084 with n=36. Repair by pairing the monthly rate 0.084/12 with 36 monthly periods.

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
