## Mastery objective

Choose and use straight-line or reducing-balance depreciation from contextual evidence.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-03

Mastery objective: Choose and use straight-line or reducing-balance depreciation from contextual evidence.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

Straight-line depreciation removes a fixed amount based on the original cost each year. Reducing-balance depreciation removes a fixed percentage of the current value. State the model before substituting because the two methods can produce very different values.

Formula and notation anchor  Straight-line: A=P(1-in). Reducing balance: A=P(1-i)^n.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Place purchase price at date 0 and mark each year. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Show equal rand decreases for straight-line depreciation. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Show shrinking rand decreases for reducing balance. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Select the formula from the wording and substitute with brackets. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify A<P and that the value is sensible and non-negative. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: A vehicle costing R480 000 depreciates by 18% p.a. reducing balance for 5 years.

Worked method: A=480 000(0.82)^5≈R177 955.12.

Question: Equipment costing R75 000 loses 12% of original cost each year for 6 years.

Worked method: A=75 000(1−0.12×6)=R21 000.

Misconception repair  Mixing straight-line and reducing-balance rules. Repair by asking: is the percentage applied to original cost or current value?

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
