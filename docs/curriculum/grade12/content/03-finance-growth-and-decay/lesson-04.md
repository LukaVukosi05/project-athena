## Mastery objective

Convert fluently between nominal and effective annual rates for monthly, quarterly and semi-annual compounding.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-04

Mastery objective: Convert fluently between nominal and effective annual rates for monthly, quarterly and semi-annual compounding.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A nominal rate names an annual rate together with a compounding frequency. An effective annual rate measures the actual percentage change over one year. Compare financial products only after expressing their rates over the same time interval.

Formula and notation anchor  i_eff=(1+j/m)^m−1;  j=m[(1+i_eff)^(1/m)−1].

| 5 | Verify conversion by reversing the formula. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Find the effective annual rate for 10.2% p.a. compounded monthly.

Worked method: i_eff=(1+0.102/12)^12−1≈0.106906, or 10.69%.

Question: Find the nominal rate compounded quarterly equivalent to 12% effective.

Worked method: j=4[(1.12)^(1/4)−1]≈0.11495, or 11.50% p.a. nominal.

Misconception repair  Dividing an effective rate by 12. Repair by recognising that effective rates already include within-year compounding.

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
