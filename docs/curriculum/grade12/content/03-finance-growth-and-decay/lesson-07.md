## Mastery objective

Recognise an annuity and determine whether payments occur at the beginning or end of periods.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-07

Mastery objective: Recognise an annuity and determine whether payments occur at the beginning or end of periods.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

An annuity is a series of equal payments made at equal intervals while interest compounds over compatible intervals. Standard school formulas usually model payments at the end of each period. A timeline must show the first payment, last payment and valuation date.

Formula and notation anchor  Annuity-immediate: payments at t=1,2,…,n; no payment at t=0.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw t=0 to t=n with every payment arrow. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Highlight that the first payment is at t=1. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Count payment arrows rather than gaps only. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Show how each deposit earns a different number of periods. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify the formula timing against a three-payment expansion. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Three annual deposits of R1 000 begin one year from now. Write the value at t=3.

Worked method: 1 000(1+i)^2+1 000(1+i)+1 000.

Question: If the first of 24 monthly payments is made today, what changes?

Worked method: It is an annuity-due; shift each ordinary-annuity payment one period forward, multiplying the immediate value by (1+i).

Misconception repair  Using n as the number of gaps drawn instead of the number of equal payments. Repair by counting the arrows and locating t=0.

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
