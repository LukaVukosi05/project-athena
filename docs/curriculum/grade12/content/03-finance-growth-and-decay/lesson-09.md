## Mastery objective

Calculate the present value of regular future payments.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-09

Mastery objective: Calculate the present value of regular future payments.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A present-value annuity discounts each end-of-period payment to date 0. It models the amount that can be financed by a fixed instalment or the present worth of an income stream.

Formula and notation anchor  P=x[(1−(1+i)^−n)/i].

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw future payments from t=1 to t=n. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Move each payment backward to t=0. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Show the first payment discounted once and the last n times. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Group the geometric series into the PV formula. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify by checking that P is below total undiscounted payments for positive interest. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Find the present value of 48 monthly payments of R3 200 at 10.5% p.a. monthly.

Worked method: P=3 200[1−(1+0.105/12)^−48]/(0.105/12)≈R124 983.50.

Question: A R150 000 loan is repaid over 5 years at 9% monthly. Find x.

Worked method: x=150 000(0.09/12)/[1−(1+0.09/12)^−60]≈R3 113.75.

Misconception repair  Using the future-value formula for a loan because payments occur in the future. Repair: identify the common valuation date—loan amount is at date 0.

## Independent practice and memo check

| Question | Answer / expected conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| PV of R2 000 monthly, 24 months, 12% nominal | R42 486.77 |
| Loan valuation date | Date 0 |
| PV compared with total payments | PV is smaller for positive interest |
| Unknown instalment isolation | Multiply P by i and divide by 1−(1+i)^−n |

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
