## Mastery objective

Model a reducing-balance loan and calculate its regular repayment.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-10

Mastery objective: Model a reducing-balance loan and calculate its regular repayment.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A loan creates a positive amount at date 0 followed by repayments in later periods. Interest is charged on the outstanding balance. Keep the lender/borrower sign convention consistent, but use magnitudes when the question asks for a positive instalment.

Formula and notation anchor  Loan: P=x[(1−(1+i)^−n)/i].

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Draw the loan inflow at t=0 and repayment outflows. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Convert annual nominal rate to the payment-period rate. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Count repayments and identify the final payment. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Solve the PV-annuity equation for x. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify instalment × n exceeds principal when interest is positive. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: A R750 000 loan is repaid monthly over 20 years at 11.2% p.a. compounded monthly.

Worked method: x=750 000(0.112/12)/[1−(1+0.112/12)^−240]≈R7 843.75.

Question: A 10% deposit is paid on a R420 000 car. Identify P for the loan.

Worked method: P=0.90×420 000=R378 000 before applying the annuity formula.

Misconception repair  Using the cash price as P after a deposit. Repair by calculating the financed balance first.

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
