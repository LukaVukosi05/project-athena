## Mastery objective

Calculate a loan balance at a specified time and state precisely whether it is before or after a payment.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-11

Mastery objective: Calculate a loan balance at a specified time and state precisely whether it is before or after a payment.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

The outstanding balance is the present value, at the chosen intermediate date, of the repayments still due. An equivalent retrospective method accumulates the original loan and subtracts the accumulated value of payments already made. Both methods must use the same valuation instant.

Formula and notation anchor  After payment k: B_k=x[(1−(1+i)^−(n−k))/i].

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mark payment k and label ‘immediately after’. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Fade paid instalments and highlight n−k remaining payments. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Move remaining payments back to date k. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Compute B_k with the PV-annuity formula. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify with the retrospective balance method. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: For the R750 000 loan at R7 843.75 monthly, find the balance after payment 60.

Worked method: 180 payments remain. B_60≈7 843.7546[1−(1+0.112/12)^−180]/(0.112/12)≈R682 548.01.

Question: How does the balance immediately before payment 60 relate to after payment 60?

Worked method: Before-payment balance equals after-payment balance plus payment 60, provided both are valued at that same instant.

Misconception repair  Using k as the number of remaining payments. Repair by writing n−k explicitly on the timeline.

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
