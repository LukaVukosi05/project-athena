## Mastery objective

Calculate regular deposits required to meet a future replacement or savings target.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-12

Mastery objective: Calculate regular deposits required to meet a future replacement or savings target.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

A sinking fund accumulates equal deposits to a future target. It is a future-value annuity. If an existing amount is already invested, move it to the target date first and subtract it from the target before calculating the regular deposits.

Formula and notation anchor  x=(target−future value of existing funds)·i/[(1+i)^n−1].

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Place the target at the final date. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Accumulate any existing investment to that date. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Subtract its future value from the target. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Show n equal deposits accumulating to the shortfall. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Verify deposits plus earned interest meet the target. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Save R300 000 with monthly deposits for 5 years at 8.5% p.a. monthly.

Worked method: x=300 000(0.085/12)/[(1+0.085/12)^60−1]≈R4 029.96.

Question: A fund already has R40 000, which will grow to R55 000 by the target date. Target R250 000.

Worked method: The annuity must accumulate R195 000, not R250 000.

Misconception repair  Subtracting the current existing balance from the future target. Repair by moving both amounts to the same date before subtracting.

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
