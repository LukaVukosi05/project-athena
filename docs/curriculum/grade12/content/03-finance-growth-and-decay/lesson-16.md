## Mastery objective

Demonstrate independent mastery across rates, growth, depreciation, annuities, loans and multi-stage timelines.

Duration: 50–65 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-finance-16

Mastery objective: Demonstrate independent mastery across rates, growth, depreciation, annuities, loans and multi-stage timelines.

Mastery threshold: 80% with targeted remediation

## Teach the idea

The assessment mirrors the reasoning demands of Paper 1 without copying a paper. It awards method for timeline construction, compatible rate and period choices, correct formula selection, substitution, computation, interpretation and verification.

Formula and notation anchor  Assessment target: approximately 15 marks, mixed across knowledge, routine, complex and problem-solving demand.

## Whiteboard sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Display the assessment map and mark allocation. | Orient and identify the focal date. |
| 2 | Require an annotated timeline before high-value calculations. | Predict the required operation. |
| 3 | Lock full solutions during the first attempt. | Calculate with visible units. |
| 4 | Reveal the memo one reasoning line at a time after submission. | Explain why the state is equivalent. |
| 5 | Route scores below 80% to lessons tagged by the first invalid step. | Verify and summarise. |

## Worked examples

Question: Integrated item: loan balance after a rate change.

Worked method: Split the timeline, value the old balance at the change date, then apply the new annuity conditions.

Question: Error item: learner uses 0.12 with 60 monthly periods.

Worked method: First invalid step is the unmatched rate; use i=0.12/12 with n=60.

Misconception repair  Treating a correct calculator output as evidence of mastery. Repair by requiring the model, substitutions and interpretation.

## Progressive support

| Support stage | Learner-facing action |
| --- | --- |
| Hint 1 — strategy | Draw or inspect the timeline. Name the focal date and decide whether values move forward or backward. |
| Hint 2 — structure | Write the periodic rate i and period/payment count n with units before selecting the formula. |
| Hint 3 — checkpoint | Substitute without rounding, calculate, then test direction, magnitude, timing and units against the context. |

## Worked-solution presentation

- Reveal the model and timeline before the numerical result.

- Give every algebraic line a plain-language ‘Why?’ explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt.

- End with a financial interpretation and an independent verification.

Exit checkpoint  The learner must explain—in words—why the chosen focal date, periodic rate and period count belong together. Correct calculation without this explanation is supported success, not yet independent mastery.
