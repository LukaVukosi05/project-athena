## Mastery objective

Construct and interpret a secant line as a nearby average-rate measurement.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-calculus-03

Mastery objective: Construct and interpret a secant line as a nearby average-rate measurement.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

A secant cuts a curve at two distinct points. It records average behaviour between them, not the exact behaviour at either endpoint.

Formula anchor  m_sec = [f(x+h)-f(x)]/h, with h not equal to 0.

Where it comes from and why it works  The horizontal change from x to x+h is h. Substituting these two x-values into the ordinary gradient formula creates the difference quotient.

## Whiteboard reasoning sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Place P at x and Q at x+h. | Orient: name the target quantity and what is known. |
| 2 | Highlight the horizontal separation h. | Inspect: connect symbols to graph objects or changing quantities. |
| 3 | Write the two output values. | Operate: show the cause before the changed expression. |
| 4 | Join P and Q and calculate its gradient. | Explain: state why the transition is mathematically valid. |
| 5 | Change h and observe how the secant responds. | Verify: check direction, units, coordinates, signs or boundaries. |

## Worked examples

Question: For f(x)=x^2 at x=2 with h=1.

Worked reasoning: m=[3^2-2^2]/1=5.

Question: For f(x)=x^2 at x=2 with h=0.1.

Worked reasoning: m=[2.1^2-4]/0.1=4.1; this is closer to 4.

Misconception repair  Calling a secant gradient instantaneous. Repair by pointing to the two distinct curve points used.

- Begin with a one-sentence target statement: 'I need to find ...'.

- Reveal the formula's visual meaning before using its compressed symbolic form.

- Give each algebraic line a plain-language 'Why?' explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt rather than only showing the final memo.
