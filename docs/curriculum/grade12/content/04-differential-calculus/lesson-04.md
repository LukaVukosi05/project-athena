## Mastery objective

Explain how the limiting position of secants produces the tangent gradient.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-calculus-04

Mastery objective: Explain how the limiting position of secants produces the tangent gradient.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

Keep one point fixed and move the second point closer. The secant lines rotate toward one limiting line: the tangent. Their gradients approach one number: the derivative at the fixed point.

Formula anchor  f'(a) = limit as h approaches 0 of [f(a+h)-f(a)]/h.

Where it comes from and why it works  The derivative is not obtained by setting h=0 in an undefined fraction. Algebra first removes the factor causing the zero denominator; the limit then evaluates the simplified expression.

## Worked examples

Question: For f(x)=x^2 at a=2.

Worked reasoning: The secant gradient simplifies to 4+h, so as h approaches 0 the gradient approaches 4.

Question: For f(x)=x^2 at a=-1.

Worked reasoning: The gradient simplifies to -2+h and approaches -2.

Misconception repair  Saying h equals zero throughout. Repair: h is non-zero during the secant calculation and only approaches zero in the limit.
