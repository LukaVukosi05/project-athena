## Mastery objective

Explain why a constant differentiates to zero and distinguish constants from coefficients.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-calculus-10

Mastery objective: Explain why a constant differentiates to zero and distinguish constants from coefficients.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

A constant function has no output change as x changes. Its graph is horizontal, so every secant and tangent gradient is zero.

Formula anchor  d/dx(c)=0; d/dx(ax)=a.

Where it comes from and why it works  From first principles, [c-c]/h=0 for every non-zero h. A coefficient multiplying x is not a constant term: ax changes with x.

## Worked examples

Question: Differentiate 7.

Worked reasoning: 0, because the output never changes.

Question: Differentiate 7x+4.

Worked reasoning: 7; 7x changes at 7 units per x-unit, while 4 contributes zero.

Misconception repair  Deleting every number, including coefficients. Repair by asking whether the number stands alone or scales a changing x-term.
