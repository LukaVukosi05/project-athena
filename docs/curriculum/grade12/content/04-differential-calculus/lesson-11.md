## Mastery objective

Differentiate polynomial sums term by term and explain why rates add and subtract.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-calculus-11

Mastery objective: Differentiate polynomial sums term by term and explain why rates add and subtract.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

If an output is built by adding component outputs, its change is the sum of their changes. Differentiation therefore distributes across addition and subtraction.

Formula anchor  d/dx[f(x)+g(x)] = f'(x)+g'(x); d/dx[f(x)-g(x)] = f'(x)-g'(x).

Where it comes from and why it works  The difference quotient of a sum separates algebraically into two difference quotients. Each approaches its own derivative, so their limits combine.

## Whiteboard reasoning sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Colour each polynomial term. | Orient: name the target quantity and what is known. |
| 2 | Apply the correct local rule to one term at a time. | Inspect: connect symbols to graph objects or changing quantities. |
| 3 | Preserve every plus and minus sign. | Operate: show the cause before the changed expression. |
| 4 | Collect the derivative terms. | Explain: state why the transition is mathematically valid. |
| 5 | Check degree: a non-constant polynomial derivative is one degree lower. | Verify: check direction, units, coordinates, signs or boundaries. |

## Worked examples

Question: Differentiate 2x^4-3x^2+5x-7.

Worked reasoning: 8x^3-6x+5.

Question: Differentiate -x^3+4x^2-9.

Worked reasoning: -3x^2+8x.

Misconception repair  Losing a negative sign when differentiating a negative term. Repair by treating the sign as part of the coefficient.

## Detailed worked-solution presentation

- Begin with a one-sentence target statement: 'I need to find ...'.

- Reveal the formula's visual meaning before using its compressed symbolic form.

- Give each algebraic line a plain-language 'Why?' explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt rather than only showing the final memo.

- Finish with an independent verification: graph behaviour, substitution, sign analysis, units, domain or boundary comparison.

Explain-back exit checkpoint  The learner must explain why the formula or derivative condition applies here. A correct answer with no explanation is recorded as supported procedural success, not yet independent conceptual mastery.
