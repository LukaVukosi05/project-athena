## Mastery objective

Connect a derivative value to the gradient of the tangent at a specified point.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Stable lesson ID: g12-calculus-12

Mastery objective: Connect a derivative value to the gradient of the tangent at a specified point.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

The derivative function assigns a tangent gradient to every allowed x-value. Evaluating f'(a) selects the gradient at the point where x=a.

Formula anchor  Tangent gradient at x=a: m=f'(a).

Where it comes from and why it works  Differentiate first because f'(x) describes local slopes. Substitute a afterward because the question asks for one particular local slope.

## Whiteboard reasoning sequence

| Frame | Board state and animation | Narration / learner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Plot the point P(a,f(a)). | Orient: name the target quantity and what is known. |
| 2 | Differentiate the whole function. | Inspect: connect symbols to graph objects or changing quantities. |
| 3 | Substitute a into f'(x). | Operate: show the cause before the changed expression. |
| 4 | Rotate a tangent until its gradient matches. | Explain: state why the transition is mathematically valid. |
| 5 | Check whether its sign agrees with the curve rising or falling. | Verify: check direction, units, coordinates, signs or boundaries. |

## Worked examples

Question: f(x)=x^3-3x at x=2.

Worked reasoning: f'(x)=3x^2-3, so m=f'(2)=9.

Question: f(x)=x^2 at x=-1.

Worked reasoning: m=2(-1)=-2, matching a falling tangent.

Misconception repair  Substituting x=a into f before differentiating and reporting f(a) as the gradient. Repair by separating point height from slope.

## Detailed worked-solution presentation

- Begin with a one-sentence target statement: 'I need to find ...'.

- Reveal the formula's visual meaning before using its compressed symbolic form.

- Give each algebraic line a plain-language 'Why?' explanation.

- Highlight the first invalid step when repairing an attempt rather than only showing the final memo.

- Finish with an independent verification: graph behaviour, substitution, sign analysis, units, domain or boundary comparison.

Explain-back exit checkpoint  The learner must explain why the formula or derivative condition applies here. A correct answer with no explanation is recorded as supported procedural success, not yet independent conceptual mastery.
