## Mastery objective

Determine the equation of a tangent using the derivative and a point on the curve.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Mastery objective: Determine the equation of a tangent using the derivative and a point on the curve.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

A tangent equation needs two ingredients: its gradient and one point. Differentiation supplies the gradient; the original function supplies the point.

Formula anchor  y-y1 = m(x-x1), where m=f'(x1) and y1=f(x1).

Where it comes from and why it works  Point-gradient form encodes all lines through (x1,y1) with gradient m. Substituting the derivative gradient selects the tangent rather than another line through the point.

## Worked examples

Question: Find the tangent to f(x)=x^2 at x=2.

Worked reasoning: Point (2,4), m=4; y-4=4(x-2), so y=4x-4.

Question: Tangent to f(x)=x^3-3x at x=1.

Worked reasoning: Point (1,-2), m=0; y=-2.

Misconception repair  Using f(x1) as the gradient. Repair by placing f(x1) and f'(x1) in two labelled boxes: height and slope.
