## Mastery objective

Determine intervals of concave-up and concave-down behaviour from the second derivative.

Duration: 50-70 minutes

Mastery objective: Determine intervals of concave-up and concave-down behaviour from the second derivative.

Mastery threshold: 80% with conceptual explanation required

## Teach the idea before the formula

Concavity describes how tangent gradients evolve. If gradients increase, the graph bends upward; if gradients decrease, it bends downward.

Formula anchor  f''(x)>0: concave up; f''(x)<0: concave down.

Where it comes from and why it works  The sign of f'' is read over intervals, because concavity is behaviour across a region rather than a single coordinate.

## Worked examples

Question: For f=x^3-3x^2, find concavity intervals.

Worked reasoning: f''=6x-6. Concave down for x<1 and up for x>1.

Question: Why not use the sign of f'?

Worked reasoning: f' tells rising/falling; f'' tells how the slope changes.

Misconception repair  Calling every decreasing region concave down. Repair by separating slope sign from slope change.
