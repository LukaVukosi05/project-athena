## Assessment implications

- First-principles items must expose substitution, expansion, a common factor and legal cancellation as separate mark-bearing decisions.
- Tangent items require both the derivative gradient and a point from the original function.
- Cubic chains combine parameters, stationary coordinates, sketches and concavity rather than testing isolated facts only.
- Motion questions require differentiated quantities, signed interpretation and units.
- Athena’s ordinary questions remain original and are labelled `Athena-authored`; an exact DBE item would require year, paper, question and memo provenance.
