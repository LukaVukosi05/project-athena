# Topic mastery and engineering acceptance

# 5. Topic mastery assessment

The final assessment must resemble the demand profile of a national Paper 1 algebra question without copying a past paper. It is completed before hints or worked solutions become available.

| Section | Marks | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Quadratic solving | 5 | One factorable quadratic and one formula item with correct standard form and rounding. |
| B. Inequality | 4 | Critical values, sign reasoning, interval notation and endpoint treatment. |
| C. Surd or quadratic-form equation | 4 | Domain/structure, valid transformation, all candidates and original-equation check. |
| D. Simultaneous equations | 5 | Substitution/elimination, standard form, all paired solutions and verification. |
| E. Exponential or parameter reasoning | 4 | Correct exponent/discriminant condition and interpretation. |
| F. Algebraic justification | 3 | A concise general argument, not numerical trial only. |
| Total | 25 | CAPS-aligned Paper 1 algebra evidence. |

| TOPIC PASS Minimum 20/25, no critical misconception, and a successful explain-back on one randomly selected solution. After targeted repair, the learner completes a parallel reassessment rather than repeating the same paper. |

# 6. Mastery analytics and reteaching

| Tag | Observed error | Required repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STANDARD-FORM | Uses a method before writing ax^2+bx+c=0. | Rebuild equation state and identify a, b and c. |
| SIGN/BRACKETS | Drops a negative sign or substitutes without brackets. | Token-level sign tracing and calculator-entry comparison. |
| ROOT-LOSS | Finds one factor/root but omits another. | Zero-product branch map and original-equation check. |
| INEQUALITY/INTERVAL | Reports roots instead of intervals or mishandles endpoints. | Graph/number-line region test with sample points. |
| SURD/EXTRANEOUS | Accepts every root after squaring. | Original-equation verification and domain check. |
| K-METHOD | Solves for k but does not return to x. | Two-stage solution tree with real-domain filtering. |
| SIMULTANEOUS/PAIRING | Mixes x- and y-values from different branches. | Ordered-pair recovery and dual-equation substitution. |
| DISCRIMINANT | Solves for x instead of applying a root condition. | Map language to Delta >0, =0 or <0. |
| RESTRICTION | Cancels a factor and restores an excluded value. | State denominator restrictions before simplification. |
| CONTEXT | Reports a mathematically valid but impossible answer. | Units, sign, domain and contextual conclusion checklist. |

# 7. Engineering and content acceptance checks

- Topic 5 is added after Differential Calculus using the existing topic-card and learning-path components.

- Topics 1–4 and every other topic remain content- and snapshot-equivalent.

- Exactly 18 Algebra lessons are registered with stable IDs and order.

- Every lesson exposes exactly 10 whiteboard examples and 12 self-guided questions with unique IDs.

- Every answer has an automated calculation check or independent mathematics-review record.

- Whiteboard operations are semantic and deterministic; no arbitrary executable content is stored.

- Self-guided answers remain hidden until submission or the configured support path permits a memo.

- The 25-mark assessment is attempted without hints and uses a fresh parallel reassessment after repair.

- ATHENA_UNLOCK_ALL_LESSONS=true unlocks every current Grade 12 topic and lesson only in local development.

- The unlock override never awards mastery, completion, subscription status or payment access and cannot operate in production.

- Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion and non-colour focus behaviour pass regression checks.
