# Algebraic Expressions, Lessons 1–10

The DOCX is an authored draft aligned to the DBE CAPS Grades 7–9 Mathematics Algebraic Expressions scope. Its 15 original examples, practice, assessment and reassessment records are extracted into `backend/app/curriculum/grade8/generated/algebraic_expressions_01.json`. The checked-in SHA-256 matches the source. The runtime has no AI-generated questions.

Lessons `g8-algebraic-expressions-01` through `-03` are registered. Lesson 2 adds and subtracts like terms; Lesson 3 multiplies monomials. Each has a separate DOCX source, generated data, 15 worked examples, 15 practice questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three parallel reassessments. The learner endpoint serves a neutral preview; the requested example is revealed through its dedicated endpoint. Practice and mastery answers remain in private server-side banks. The terminal whiteboard state checks the original question.

Lesson 3 has five new visual families: factor arrays, sign grids, area products, factor stacks and side-by-side area comparison. Question-specific factors and scene dimensions determine the diagrams. Lessons 1–3 retain teacher-review status; this is not a claim that the Grade 8 topic is finished. Lesson 3 still needs human desktop and mobile browser acceptance before changing its review label. The existing short-answer grading checks authored strings, so alternative mathematically equivalent responses need more work before general release.

Lesson 4 is an original source draft aligned to the CAPS multiplication of integers and monomials by binomials and trinomials. It contains 15 worked expansions, 15 independent questions, five mastery questions and three parallel reassessments. Its question-specific visuals show signed paths and split areas. It remains under teacher review. Human Windows browser acceptance and independent mathematical review are still required. It does not complete the Grade 8 Algebraic Expressions topic.

Lesson 5 follows with division of monomials, binomials and trinomials by an integer or monomial. Its separate source pack and runtime contain 15 authored worked examples, 15 independent questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three reassessments. Quotient lanes show each signed term separately; variable divisors preserve the zero exclusion. It awaits teacher review, alternative-answer scoring work and learner browser acceptance.

Lesson 6 follows the Grade 8 CAPS and 2026 ATP with squares, cubes, square roots and cube roots of algebraic terms. Its separate original source pack and runtime contain 15 worked examples, 15 independent questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three reassessments. The board groups two or three complete factors, distinguishes signs inside and outside an exponent, and displays square-root factor pairs, negative-input checks and parallel root branches. Unrestricted square roots use the nonnegative magnitude, for example √(a²)=|a|. It awaits teacher review, alternative-answer scoring work and learner browser acceptance.

Lesson 7 teaches numerical substitution as specified by the Grade 8 ATP. Its original source pack and runtime contain 15 worked examples, 15 independent questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three reassessments. The visual board labels each input slot, brackets negative values, maintains separate paths for multiple variables and equivalent expressions, marks denominator-zero cases, and evaluates rectangle perimeter with the question's dimensions. It awaits teacher review, alternative-answer scoring and learner browser acceptance.

Lesson 8 integrates expansion, signed bracket subtraction, termwise quotient and like-term collection. Its 15 original worked examples, 15 practice questions, five mastery questions and three reassessments remain under teacher review. Visuals preserve the input groups, reveal derived values only after their operations, and mark a nonzero variable divisor where relevant. Human browser acceptance and algebraic equivalent-answer scoring remain open.

Lesson 9 models verbal rules, input-output tables, flow diagrams, money, volume, area and perimeter, paired plans and error diagnosis. The original source contains 15 worked examples, 15 independent questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three reassessments. It remains under teacher review; human browser acceptance and mathematically equivalent short-answer scoring remain open.

Lesson 10 is an authored consolidation of algebraic expressions and first-error diagnosis aligned to the Grade 8 CAPS/2026 ATP scope. Its source pack contains 15 distinct worked examples, 15 practice questions, five mastery questions totalling 20 marks and three parallel reassessments. Question-specific diagnostic boards keep the original claim and prior states visible, reveal operations before results and hide the answer until the teaching step. It remains under teacher review. Equivalent-answer scoring and human desktop/mobile acceptance remain open.
