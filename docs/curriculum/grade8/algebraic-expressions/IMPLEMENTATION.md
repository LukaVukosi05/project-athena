# Algebraic Expressions, Lesson 1

The DOCX is an authored draft aligned to the DBE CAPS Grades 7–9 Mathematics Algebraic Expressions scope. Its 15 original examples, practice, assessment and reassessment records are extracted into `backend/app/curriculum/grade8/generated/algebraic_expressions_01.json`. The checked-in SHA-256 matches the source. The runtime has no AI-generated questions.

`g8-algebraic-expressions-01` is the only registered lesson for this topic. Its topic and lesson explicitly require teacher review; this is not a claim that all Grade 8 Algebraic Expressions material is complete. Its 15 whiteboard questions use source-specific states, captions and semantic targets. The learner endpoint serves only a neutral preview; the requested example is revealed through its dedicated endpoint. Practice and mastery answers live in private server-side banks. The terminal whiteboard state checks the original question.

The current models are authored term objects, an area grid, a fraction bar, receipts and a labelled rectangle. Mathematical and mobile browser inspection is still required before changing the source/release label to approved. In particular, human review should check the legibility of signs, expression grouping, voice timing, numeric equivalence accepted by short-answer grading and the final verification step of all 15 examples.
