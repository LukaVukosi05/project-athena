# Grade 8 Common Fractions — Lesson 3: Multiplying Fractions

Original Project Athena authoring; 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1, Common Fractions.
Source: https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113
Teacher review and local learner browser acceptance required before release.

## Teaching design

For a fraction of a whole, show equal groups, then select the required groups. For two fractions, overlay equal-unit strips and show the product as an area. Convert mixed numbers first. Multiply the numerators and denominators and check by a reverse division.

Each example has a five-stage board: identify operands, establish equal units, perform the visible operation, simplify, and verify. The answer is hidden in the initial state.

### g8-frac-l03-w01
Question: Find 3/4 of 20 counters.
Visual: group/area model of 3/4 and 20; labels and selected portions use these values.
1. Interpret 3/4 × 20: start with 20 as one quantity and select 3/4 of it.
2. Convert to improper fractions when needed: 3/4 × 20/1.
3. Multiply the selected parts: (3 × 20)/(4 × 1) = 60/4; simplify to 15.
4. Answer: 15.
5. 3/4 × 20 = 15. Check the whole by dividing 15 by 3/4 to recover 20.
Misconception repair: Do not multiply only one numerator; preserve each whole and convert mixed numbers before multiplying.
