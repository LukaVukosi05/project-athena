# Grade 8 Common Fractions — Lesson 5: Powers and roots of common fractions

Original Athena authoring based on the 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1.
Source: https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113

Teacher mathematical review and learner browser acceptance required before release.

The whiteboard starts with a problem and values only. The first event builds the original quantity, the second constructs equal units, the third performs the operation, the fourth simplifies, and the fifth checks against the untouched question.

### g8-frac-l05-w01
Question: Find the square of 2/3.
Visual: fraction_power with the current question’s values and units. Answer concealed initially.
1. Start with 2/3 as a part of the same whole. The exponent 2 means 2 identical factors.
2. Convert 2/3 to 2/3; show 2 copies, each with its own numerator and denominator.
3. Multiply across all 2 factors: 2^2/3^2 = 4/9 = 4/9.
4. Give exact simplified answer: 4/9.
5. Check: the product of 2 copies of 2/3 equals 4/9; the unit squares model counts both numerator and denominator factors.
Misconception repair: The exponent or root acts on both numerator and denominator; a root cannot be made by dividing them by the index.
