# Grade 8 Common Fractions — Lesson 6: Fraction problems in context

Original Athena authoring based on the 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1.
Source: https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113

Teacher mathematical review and learner browser acceptance required before release.

The whiteboard starts with a problem and values only. The first event builds the original quantity, the second constructs equal units, the third performs the operation, the fourth simplifies, and the fifth checks against the untouched question.

### g8-frac-l06-w01
Question: Three quarters of 24 learners joined the maths club. How many joined?
Visual: fraction_context with the current question’s values and units. Answer concealed initially.
1. Name the original unit (learners) and identify the two amounts: 3/4 and 24.
2. Select 3/4 of 24; divide the whole into equal parts and count the selected groups.
3. Calculate 3/4 × 24 = 18; give the result in learners and simplify.
4. Give exact simplified answer: 18.
5. Check the original learners situation: 3/4 × 24 = 18 learners. Confirm by a reverse operation or recombining the parts.
Misconception repair: Do not lose track of the whole or its unit. Match piece sizes before counting and include partial groups.
