# Grade 8 Common Fractions Lesson 7: Percentage of a whole and part

Original authored questions and mathematical teaching states. Teacher review required before release.

The teaching sequence reveals the original whole, proportional model, calculation and verification. The final answer is withheld until the learner reaches the verification step.

## Example 1: Express 1/4 as a percentage.
**Answer:** 25%
**Whiteboard:** Mark 1 as the whole and partition it into 100 equal cells; shade the same share as 1/4. The second row converts that share to hundredths and the third divides the rate by 100 to check 1/4. Keep each earlier state visible and withhold the final answer until verification.
1. The whole is one, or 100%. Keep the fraction 1/4 of that whole.
2. Multiply 1/4 by 100%: 1/4 × 100% = 25%.
3. Check: 25 ÷ 100 = 1/4.
**Verification:** The whole is one, or 100%. Keep the fraction 1/4 of that whole. Multiply 1/4 by 100%: 1/4 × 100% = 25%. Check: 25 ÷ 100 = 1/4. Reverse check using the original whole 1.
