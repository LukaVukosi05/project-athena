# Grade 8 Common Fractions Lesson 8: Percentage increase and decrease

Original authored questions and mathematical teaching states. Teacher review required before release.

The teaching sequence reveals the original whole, proportional model, calculation and verification. The final answer is withheld until the learner reaches the verification step.

## Example 1: Increase 80 by 10%. What is the new amount?
**Answer:** 88
**Whiteboard:** Hold the original 80 bar fixed at 100%. Build a second bar, mark the 10% change against the original, then extend the new bar by that amount. Reveal the new total after the operation. Keep each earlier state visible and withhold the final answer until verification.
1. The original amount is 80; it represents 100%.
2. Calculate 10% of 80: 80 × 10/100 = 8.
3. Add the change: 80 + 8 = 88.
**Verification:** The original amount is 80; it represents 100%. Calculate 10% of 80: 80 × 10/100 = 8. Add the change: 80 + 8 = 88. Reverse check using the original whole 80.

## Example 5: The value changes from 100 to 120. Find the percentage change and state its direction.
**Answer:** 20% increase
**Whiteboard:** Place an original bar at 100 and a new bar at 120; mark their signed difference. Divide the difference by the ORIGINAL 100, not the new 120, then show the percentage and direction. Keep each earlier state visible and withhold the final answer until verification.
1. The ORIGINAL amount is 100, the new amount is 120.
2. The increase is |120 − 100| = 20.
3. Divide change by ORIGINAL amount: 20 ÷ 100 × 100% = 20% increase.
**Verification:** The ORIGINAL amount is 100, the new amount is 120. The increase is |120 − 100| = 20. Divide change by ORIGINAL amount: 20 ÷ 100 × 100% = 20% increase. Reverse check using the original whole 100.
