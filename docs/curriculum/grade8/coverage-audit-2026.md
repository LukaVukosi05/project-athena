3. Mixed angle diagrams: choose and justify each relationship. Whiteboard: preserve the diagram, mark the relevant pair, state the theorem, derive one equation, then reveal the next angle.
4. Unknowns in multi-step parallel-line diagrams. Whiteboard: trace transversal and parallel marks, solve for the variable and substitute into the requested angle.

## Topic check and limits

All 13 registered topics now offer a **revision check**. The existing Functions check uses its 18 purpose-written items. Other topics sample two **existing lesson mastery items** from every authored lesson; the check therefore tests routing and recall, and must not be presented as unseen or independent mastery evidence. Answers and worked review are withheld until submission. A weak result links directly to the relevant lesson. Each lesson's separate mastery, repair and parallel reassessment flow remains authoritative. Repeating the topic check reuses its fixed items.

No grade-wide readiness claim is justified while five ATP topic areas and the named scope above are absent. Completion requires content creation, teacher mathematical review, browser inspection on desktop and mobile, and learner outcome evidence.
