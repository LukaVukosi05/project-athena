# Grade 8 Decimal Fractions Lesson 2: Rounding and estimating decimals

Original Athena examples. Curriculum scope: 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1 (https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113). Questions and worked explanations are original rather than reproduced from the ATP. Teacher review required.

A learner sees the question first. Digit columns, equal place values and number-line marks appear one operation at a time. The final answer is hidden until verification.

## Worked example 1: Round 3.476 to 2 decimal places.
Whiteboard: Draw adjacent marks with 2 decimal places around 3.476; reveal the midpoint, original dot and then the rounded endpoint. Hold each prior mathematical state on the board.
1. Neighbouring marks at 2 decimal place(s) are 3.47 and 3.48.
2. The midpoint is 3.475. Place 3.476 relative to it; a midpoint rounds upward.
3. Round to 3.48, retaining 2 decimal place(s).
Answer (revealed after reasoning): 3.48
Check: 3.476 is at or beyond the midpoint 3.475
