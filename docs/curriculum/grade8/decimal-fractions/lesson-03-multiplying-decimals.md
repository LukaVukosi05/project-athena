# Grade 8 Decimal Fractions Lesson 3: Multiplying decimal fractions

Original Athena questions; aligned to the 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1 (https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113). Teacher review required.

Each operation has its own whiteboard state. The complete question is initially visible, but the quotient or product and later steps stay hidden until taught.

## Worked example 1: Calculate 0.4 × 0.3. Show each partial product and check the decimal point.
Whiteboard: Digit cards for 0.4 and 0.3 keep their points visible. Convert each to its integer factor; add one aligned partial-product strip per digit in 0.3, sum, then shift the point by both original place counts. Only the current operation is highlighted; earlier results remain in view.
1. Rewrite 0.4 as 4 ÷ 10^1; the decimal point sets the first scale.
2. Rewrite 0.3 as 3 ÷ 10^1; both scales combine to 10^2.
3. Multiply 4 × 3 × 10^0 = 12; align this partial product in its place-value column.
4. Add the aligned partial products: 12 = 12.
5. Divide 12 by 10^2; place the decimal point 2 digits from the right: 0.12.
Answer (after verification): 0.12
Check: 0.12 ÷ 0.3 = 0.4, so the product agrees with both original decimal factors.
