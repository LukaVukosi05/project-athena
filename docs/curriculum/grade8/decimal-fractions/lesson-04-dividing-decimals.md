Original Athena questions; aligned to the 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1 (https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113). Teacher review required.

Each operation has its own whiteboard state. The complete question is initially visible, but the quotient or product and later steps stay hidden until taught.

## Worked example 1: Divide 1.2 by 0.3. Show the equal scaling and quotient digits.
Whiteboard: Show dividend 1.2 and divisor 0.3, scale both by the same power of ten, and build the quotient from each brought-down digit and remainder. Mark quotient decimal digits separately. Only the current operation is highlighted; earlier results remain in view.
1. Choose 10^1 so multiplying BOTH original operands clears both decimal points.
2. Apply the same factor: 1.2 ÷ 0.3 = 12 ÷ 3. The quotient is unchanged.
3. Bring down 1: 1 ÷ 3 gives digit 0; subtract 0 × 3 to leave remainder 1.
4. Bring down 2: 12 ÷ 3 gives digit 4; subtract 4 × 3 to leave remainder 0.
5. Read the quotient digits with the decimal point: 4.
Answer (after verification): 4
Check: 4 × 0.3 = 1.2, so division reverses to the original dividend.
