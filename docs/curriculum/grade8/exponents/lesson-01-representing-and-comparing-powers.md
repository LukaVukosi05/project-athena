# Grade 8 Exponents · Lesson 1: Representing and comparing powers

Scope: 2026 DBE Grade 8 Mathematics ATP Term 2, Exponents. All questions and explanations below are original. Teacher review pending.

## Objective
Represent whole and integer bases in exponent form, compare powers and write large whole numbers in scientific notation with positive exponents.

## Why it matters
Powers compress repeated multiplication. Showing the equal factors makes exponent laws explainable rather than rules to memorise.

## Intuition and prerequisites
The exponent counts factors. Negative bases need brackets; scientific notation preserves the whole number by compensating each decimal shift. Before Lesson 2, understand expanded powers and equal factor cancellation.

## Whiteboard playback
Show the complete problem first; hide the answer. Reveal the base and exponent, then one factor, group or decimal shift at a time. Preserve every completed row in the written solution beside the visual. Highlight the current operation and verify against the original problem at the end. Replay and Previous must reconstruct identical states.

**Answer:** 8
**Full memo:**
1. Identify the base 2 and exponent 3; the exponent counts equal factors.
2. Expand into exactly 3 factors: 2 × 2 × 2.
3. Multiply the first two factors: 2 × 2 = 4.
4. Multiply by each remaining factor in order to get 8.
5. The value of the original expression is 8.
**Check:** 2 × 2 × 2 = 8; repeated multiplication checks the original power.
