# Grade 8 Topic 3 — Functions and Relationships readiness audit

Status: six runtime lessons available as verified previews; no publication decision.

## Scope against South African CAPS

The DBE Grade 8 CAPS policy (Mathematics Grades 7–9, §2.2 and Term 1/4 clarification) calls for determining input values, output values and rules using flow diagrams, tables, formulae and equations; it also calls for explaining equivalent verbal, flow, table, formula and equation descriptions. The Term 4 clarification specifically uses formulas to calculate outputs and includes finding a missing length or width from the area of a rectangle.

## Release blockers

1. The three DOCX lesson sources and three new Markdown lessons remain in teacher-review status. A test pass does not approve the mathematical teaching or scenarios.
2. Browser acceptance is needed for each lesson's 15 examples at desktop and mobile widths. Pay particular attention to the Lesson 3 table rows retaining previous outputs during Next, Previous and Replay.
3. The short topic check diagnoses the six lesson families but does not include all advanced contexts. Its eighteen fixed questions cannot serve as a parallel reassessment.
4. Term 4 inverse-formula practice is authored in Lesson 4; Lessons 5 and 6 add representation matching and repair. All require independent teacher review and live browser inspection before release.
5. The current lesson assessments score typed answers against a small set of literal acceptable forms. Validate equivalent expressions, units and multi-part answers fairly before a public launch.

## Proposed final acceptance

- Teacher signs off all 90 worked examples, 90 practice questions, 30 mastery items and eighteen reassessment items, including each final check.
- Each question has a question-specific visual, legal operation before result, persistent previous states and no visible final answer at initial load.
- Review all 15 examples per lesson in a running browser and test mobile, keyboard, direct URL restoration and answer gating.
- Review the authored Formulas in context and inverse-area sequence independently; do not equate automated validation with mathematical approval.
- Only after these gates pass should `release_approved` be changed and the topic called published.
