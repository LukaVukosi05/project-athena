# Grade 8 Geometry of 2D Shapes — Lesson 5: Quadrilateral and parallelogram angles

Status: original authoring draft; mathematical teacher review and device browser acceptance pending.

Curriculum: [DBE 2026 Grade 8 Mathematics ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113), Term 3, Geometry of 2D Shapes.

Objective: quadrilateral and parallelogram angles using stated geometric properties and verified reasoning.

Prerequisites: labelled vertices and sides, right and straight angles, and equality markings.

Duration: about 90 minutes. Fifteen worked examples, fifteen independent practice questions, five mastery questions and three parallel reassessments.

## Whiteboard contract

- Plot actual labelled vertices and edges from the current question; preserve aspect ratio and do not substitute a generic triangle or quadrilateral.
- Initially show the complete question and unannotated figure. Reveal a given label, then the relevant relationship, one operation, its result, and a check.
- Keep earlier written steps visible alongside the shape; do not display the final answer in the initial scene.
- Show matching equal-side markings or parallel/right-angle evidence when the question states them. Classify from stated evidence, not the apparent drawing alone.
- Previous, Next and Replay must reconstruct deterministic stages. Captions and narration use the same sentence.

## Worked examples

### Example 01 — g8-shapes-05-w01

**Problem:** Quadrilateral ABCD has angles A = 80°, B = 105° and C = 95°. Find angle D.

**Visual:** quadrilateral with question-specific vertices [[45.0, 185.0], [201.34393858099665, 185.0], [223.82437685383036, 101.1018621893686], [69.6857772991851, 45.0]], labels {'A': '80°', 'B': '105°', 'C': '95°', 'D': 'x'}.

**Whiteboard sequence:**

1. Mark A = 80°, B = 105° and C = 95°.
2. Draw a diagonal: two triangles give 180° + 180° = 360°.
3. 80° + 105° + 95° + D = 360°.
4. D = 360° − 80° − 105° − 95° = 80°.
5. Check the original figure: 80° + 105° + 95° + 80° = 360°.

**Answer:** 80°

**First-error diagnosis:** The interior angle sum for a quadrilateral is 360°; parallelograms also have equal opposite and supplementary adjacent angles.

**Targeted repair:** redraw the same type of figure with different given measures; make the learner identify the relevant property before calculating.
