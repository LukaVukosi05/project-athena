# Grade 8 Geometry of Straight Lines — Lesson 1: Angles at intersecting and perpendicular lines

Status: original Athena authoring draft; teacher review and native visual runtime required before publication.
Curriculum: [DBE 2026 Grade 8 Mathematics ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%208.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-113), Term 3, Geometry of Straight Lines.

Learning objective: identify the angle relationship in an accurately labelled diagram, justify the equation, solve for the marked quantity and verify the original diagram.

Why it matters: a visually plausible angle is not a reason. Learners must link each equality or sum to intersecting, perpendicular or parallel lines.

Prerequisites: straight angle 180°, right angle 90°, basic algebraic equations and angle notation.
Estimated duration: 90 minutes; 15 worked examples, 15 parallel independent questions to author and five mastery items to author.

## Whiteboard contract

- Draw the stated line family before any angle number. Give each ray and intersection a stable label. Parallel lines receive visible matching arrow marks.
- Show the complete question and the given angle first. Keep x and the final calculation hidden.
- Highlight exactly the two regions to which the stated theorem applies. Display that theorem before writing its equation.
- Add one mathematical line per step beside the diagram. Preserve every earlier line; highlight the active line.
- Verify by substituting into the original diagram. Previous, Next and Replay reconstruct the same labels and states.
- Captions and eventual narration must use the same teaching sentence; reduced motion preserves the reveal order.

## Worked examples

### 01 · g8-lines-01-w01 · foundation

**Question:** In the labelled diagram, two parts of a right angle include a 34° angle. Find the marked angle x. Give a reason.

**Visual:** perpendicular diagram; two separately targetable angle sectors; right_angle relationship. Given values: (34,).

**Whiteboard teaching:**

1. Reveal and explain: Locate the given 34° angle and the separate marked angle x.
2. Reveal and explain: Identify the two parts of a right angle relationship.
3. Reveal and explain: x + 34° = 90°
4. Reveal and explain: x = 90° − 34° = 56°
5. Verify against the original diagram: 56° + 34° = 90°

**Answer:** Marked angle = 56°.

**First-error diagnosis:** Do not assume two angles are equal merely because they look alike; name the line or parallel-line relationship.

**Reteaching:** redraw only the relevant angle pair with changed values; require the learner to name the relationship before writing a new equation.

### 06 · g8-lines-01-w06 · standard

**Question:** In the diagram, angles (2x + 18)° and (20)° form a straight line. Find x and the angle (2x + 18)°.

**Visual:** intersection diagram; two separately targetable angle sectors; linear_pair relationship. Given values: (2, 18, 0, 20).

**Whiteboard teaching:**

1. Reveal and explain: Mark the two distinct angle regions before writing an equation.
2. Reveal and explain: Use the fact that these angles form a straight line.
3. Reveal and explain: (2x + 18) + (20) = 180
4. Reveal and explain: (2)x + (38) = 180
5. Reveal and explain: Solve to obtain x = 71.
6. Reveal and explain: Substitute: 2x + 18 at x = 71 gives 160°.
7. Verify against the original diagram: 160° + 20° = 180°

**Answer:** x = 71; labelled angle = 160°.

**First-error diagnosis:** The unknown is x, not the angle size; substitute the solved x into the labelled expression.

**Reteaching:** redraw only the relevant angle pair with changed values; require the learner to name the relationship before writing a new equation.
