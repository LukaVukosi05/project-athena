## g8-graphs-l03-w01: Water in a bucket

**Question.** The graph shows water in a bucket. The horizontal axis measures minutes and the vertical axis measures litres. How much water is present at minute 2?
**Domain.** continuous; horizontal: minutes; vertical: litres.
**Source coordinates.** (0, 2), (1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 8).

**Board sequence**
1. Read the axes: minutes increases to the right and litres increases upward. Mark the interval 0 to 1: litres increases from 2 to 4 (+2).
2. Trace 1 to 2: litres increases from 4 to 6 (+2); keep the first interval visible.
3. Trace 2 to 3: litres increases from 6 to 8 (+2). The graph has a maximum of 8 litres at minutes 3 and a minimum of 2 litres at minutes 0.
4. Answer the question: 6. Check the labelled minutes and litres values and treat the domain as continuous.

**Full memo.** How much water is present at minute 2? The graph values are (0, 2), (1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 8). The graph has a maximum of 8 litres at minutes 3 and a minimum of 2 litres at minutes 0. Therefore 6.
**Likely error.** Confusing minutes with litres or treating a flat interval as a missing point.
**Repair.** Project each endpoint to the minutes and litres axes; compare consecutive outputs and check whether intermediate inputs are meaningful.
