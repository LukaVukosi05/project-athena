## g8-graphs-l06-w01: Water level

**Question.** Water level: the graph records litres against minutes. A learner claims: “The water level at minute 2 is 3 L.” Find the first mistake and correct it.
**Axis data.** {'x_min': 0, 'x_max': 4, 'y_min': -2, 'y_max': 9, 'x_tick': 1, 'y_tick': 2, 'x_unit': 'minutes', 'y_unit': 'litres'}
**Graph points.** [{'x': 0, 'y': 1}, {'x': 1, 'y': 3}, {'x': 2, 'y': 5}, {'x': 3, 'y': 7}]

**Whiteboard actions**
1. Read the learner claim without accepting it: The water level at minute 2 is 3 L. Name the minutes input being discussed.
2. Project that input onto the real graph. At minutes 2, the plotted value is 5 litres.
3. Check the nearby graph values: at 1 it is 3, then at 2 it is 5. The change is +2 litres.
4. The claim is incorrect. The level at minute 2 is 5 L. Check the correction against the original plotted graph and its units.

**Memo.** The claim “The water level at minute 2 is 3 L.” conflicts with the original graph [(0, 1), (1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7)]. The level at minute 2 is 5 L.
**Misconception.** The water level at minute 2 is 3 L.
**Targeted repair.** Keep the horizontal input fixed, read the matching litres, then compute the change only over the stated interval.
