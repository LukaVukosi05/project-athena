# Grade 8 Integers — Lesson 4: Powers, roots and mixed integer operations

Status: original Athena draft; teacher and local browser review required.
CAPS alignment: DBE 2026 Grade 8 Mathematics ATP, Term 1 Integers.

## Learning aim
Reason about signs using mathematical structure, then check each result in the original expression.

## Worked whiteboard examples

### g8-int-l04-w01: Integer powers and roots 1
Problem: Evaluate (−4)² in the correct order.
Visual family: powers. The initial board shows the complete expression but conceals the result.
Whiteboard steps:
1. Identify the operands and required operation in (−4)².
2. The base is the whole bracketed number −4.
3. Multiply (−4) by (−4): the two negative factors give +16.
4. Check 4² = 16 and distinguish (−4)² from −4².
5. Conclude that (−4)² = 16; check the preceding operations against the question.
Answer: 16.
Memo: (−4)² = 16. Check 4² = 16 and distinguish (−4)² from −4².
Misconception: Evaluate brackets, powers and roots before left-to-right multiplication/division and then addition/subtraction.

## Teaching and review
A wrong sign is diagnosed at the first mismatched group, inverse, exponent scope or calculation stage. Rebuild that stage, retain the previous operations, and try a parallel problem.
