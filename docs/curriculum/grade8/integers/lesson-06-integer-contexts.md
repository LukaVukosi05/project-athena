### g8-int-l06-w01: Temperature integer context 1
Question: A thermometer reads −4 °C. It warms by 9 °C, then cools by 3 °C. What is the final temperature?
Visual: temperature; show original values first and retain every earlier mathematical stage.
1. Read the complete story and identify the given signed quantities.
2. Start at −4 °C, below zero.
3. Warm by 9 °C to reach 5 °C; keep the first change visible.
4. Cool by 3 °C to reach 2 °C; check the original changes.
5. Verify against the original context: Model -4+9-3. The signed result is 2 °C. Cool by 3 °C to reach 2 °C; check the original changes.
Answer: 2.
Memo: Model -4+9-3. The signed result is 2 °C. Cool by 3 °C to reach 2 °C; check the original changes.
