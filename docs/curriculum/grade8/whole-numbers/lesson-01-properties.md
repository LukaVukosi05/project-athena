## g8-whole-l01-w01: Commutative property of addition
**Question:** Compare 18 + 27 and 27 + 18. Calculate and name the property that justifies the comparison.
**Whiteboard input:** [18, 27, 0].
1. Write 18 + 27 and identify the complete groups and operation.
2. Swap the two addends; their sum stays the same. Represent 18 + 27 with its numbers, not an unrelated picture.
3. Apply commutative property of addition: 18 + 27 corresponds to 27 + 18.
4. Calculate both sides: 18 + 27 = 45 and 27 + 18 = 45. Verify against the original question.
**Answer and check:** 18 + 27 = 27 + 18 = 45. This is commutative property of addition; the original expression gives 45.
**Misconception:** Do not change the operation, drop a factor, or treat division by zero as zero.
