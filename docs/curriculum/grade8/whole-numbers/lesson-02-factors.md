## g8-whole-l02-w01: largest prime factor of 84
**Question:** Find the largest prime factor of 84. Show its prime factorisation.
**Whiteboard input:** [84].
1. Place 84 as the factor-tree roots. A prime leaf has no smaller factors except one.
2. Split the next composite node: 84 = 2 × 42. Keep every earlier branch visible.
3. Split the next composite node: 42 = 2 × 21. Keep every earlier branch visible.
4. Split the next composite node: 21 = 3 × 7. Keep every earlier branch visible.
5. Read all prime leaves and multiply them back to the roots: 84 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7.
6. Select the largest prime leaf. The largest prime factor is 7. 7 divides 84 and is prime.
**Answer and check:** 84 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 7. Select the largest prime leaf in the factor tree. largest prime factor = 7. 7 is prime and divides 84.
**Misconception:** Confusing common minimum powers for HCF with maximum powers for LCM, or treating 1 as prime.

3. Split the next composite node: 18 = 2 × 9; 30 = 2 × 15. Keep every earlier branch visible.
4. Split the next composite node: 9 = 3 × 3; 15 = 3 × 5. Keep every earlier branch visible.
5. Read all prime leaves and multiply them back to the roots: 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3, 60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5.
6. Select the lowest shared prime powers. The HCF is 12. 12 divides each original root.
**Answer and check:** 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3, 60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5. Take only shared prime powers at their smallest counts. HCF = 12. 12 divides every original number.
