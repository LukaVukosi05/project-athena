# Grade 9 Topic 7 · Lesson 1: Translate situations into equations

Authority: [DBE 2026 Mathematics Grade 9 ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf), Term 2. Questions and scenarios are original Athena content.

Status: teacher and browser review required. Worked examples show each intermediate equality and a substitution check. Practice is independent and four-option.

## g9-eq-l01-w01: A taxi group has 3 equal groups of learners and 4 helpers, 13 people altogether. Let x be the number in each group. Form an equation and find x.

1. Draw 3 equal group boxes labelled x and a separate box of 4 helpers.

2. The total gives 3x + 4 = 13.

3. Subtract 4 from both sides: 3x = 9.

4. Divide both sides by 3: x = 3.

5. Check: 3(3) + 4 = 13.

**Answer:** x = 3 learners per group. **Misconception:** Keep helpers outside the equal groups; the unknown counts one group.
