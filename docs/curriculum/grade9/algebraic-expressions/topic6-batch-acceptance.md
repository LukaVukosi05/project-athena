# Grade 9 Topic 6 · Algebraic Expressions

**Status: authored preview.** Nine lessons span Term 1 and Term 2. The [DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) requires common and decimal fractions in expressions, monomial and binomial products, polynomial division, substitution, common factors, difference of squares, trinomials and algebraic fractions. Athena's lesson titles, questions and visual design are original product decisions.

Each lesson has 15 worked examples, 15 separate four-option practice items, five mastery items and three changed-value reassessment items. The generated acceptance manifest records IDs, source hashes and the two still-false acceptance flags. Source documents include every question and answer key. The runtime keeps final worked answers off the initial API payload until explicit reveal; its visual scene retains the question's terms and prior worked steps. Fractions appear stacked in the model, with exact values in the written solution.
