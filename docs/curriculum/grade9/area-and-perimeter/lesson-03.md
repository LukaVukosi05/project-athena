## g9-area-l03-w01: A circular sign has radius 4 cm. Give its diameter, circumference and area exactly in terms of π; also round C and A to two decimal places.

Visual: {"family": "area", "values": {"lesson": 3, "length": 9, "width": 7, "model": "circle", "radius": 4, "diameter": 8, "circumference_coefficient": 8, "area_coefficient": 16}}

1. Mark the centre and radius r = 4 cm.

2. Diameter d = 2r = 8 cm.

3. Circumference C = 2πr = 8π cm ≈ 25.13 cm.

4. Area A = πr² = 16π cm² ≈ 50.27 cm².

5. Check: C = πd = 8π cm and A = π(4)² = 16π cm²; length uses cm, area uses cm².

**Answer:** d = 8 cm; C = 8π cm ≈ 25.13 cm; A = 16π cm² ≈ 50.27 cm². **Misconception:** Keep the given lengths and units attached to the correct edges; perimeter counts boundary length, area counts square units.
