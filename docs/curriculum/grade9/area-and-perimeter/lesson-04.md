## g9-area-l04-w01: A 9 cm by 7 cm rectangle is changed by doubling only the length. Find the new perimeter and area.

Visual: {"family": "area", "values": {"lesson": 4, "length": 9, "width": 7, "model": "scaled_rectangle", "mode": "length_only", "factor": 2, "scaled_length": 18, "scaled_width": 7, "old_perimeter": 32, "old_area": 63, "new_perimeter": 50, "new_area": 126}}

1. Mark the original dimensions 9 cm and 7 cm; double only the length.

2. The new dimensions are 18 cm and 7 cm.

3. Original perimeter = 2(9 + 7) = 32 cm; new perimeter = 2(18 + 7) = 50 cm.

4. Original area = 9 × 7 = 63 cm²; new area = 18 × 7 = 126 cm².

5. Check: area changes by 2 times; the boundary changes from 32 cm to 50 cm.

**Answer:** P = 50 cm; A = 126 cm². **Misconception:** Keep the given lengths and units attached to the correct edges; perimeter counts boundary length, area counts square units.
