## g9-area-l05-w01: A 9 m by 7 m garden has a uniform 2 m path outside all four sides. Find the path area and outer fence length.

Visual: {"family": "area", "values": {"lesson": 5, "length": 9, "width": 7, "model": "garden_path", "path": 2, "outer_length": 13, "outer_width": 11, "path_area": 80, "fence": 48}}

1. Mark the inner 9 m × 7 m garden and a path width of 2 m on each side.

2. Outer dimensions are 9 + 2(2) = 13 m and 7 + 2(2) = 11 m.

3. Outer area = 13 × 11 = 143 m²; inner area = 9 × 7 = 63 m².

4. Path area = 143 − 63 = 80 m²; outer fence length = 2(13 + 11) = 48 m.

5. Check: the path area plus the garden area equals 143 m²; the outer four sides total 48 m.

**Answer:** path = 80 m²; fence = 48 m. **Misconception:** Keep the given lengths and units attached to the correct edges; perimeter counts boundary length, area counts square units.
