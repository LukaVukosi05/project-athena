## g9-data-l01-w01: A school has 97 Grade 9 learners. A researcher asks 13 of them about travel to school. State the population and sample sizes.

Visual: {"family": "data", "values": {"lesson": 1, "model": "survey", "population": 97, "sample": 13}}

1. Identify the question: travel for the 97 Grade 9 learners.

2. The population is everyone the research describes: 97 learners.

3. The sample is the smaller group actually asked: 13 learners.

4. The 13 replies must not be reported as 97 individual replies.

5. Check: 13 < 97; state both sizes and who belongs to each group.

**Answer:** population = 97; sample = 13. **Misconception:** Keep the source, count, denominator and axis scale tied to the original dataset.
