## g9-data-l04-w01: The seven daily counts are 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 21. Find the mean (two decimals), median, range and possible high outlier.

Visual: {"family": "data", "values": {"lesson": 4, "model": "dotplot", "values": [5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 21], "total": 71, "median": 9, "mean_rounded": "10.14", "range": 16, "outlier": 21}}

1. Arrange the seven counts in order: 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 21.

2. Sum them: 5 + 7 + 8 + 9 + 10 + 11 + 21 = 71.

3. Mean = 71 ÷ 7 ≈ 10.14; the fourth value gives median 9.

4. Range = 21 − 5 = 16; 21 lies well above the cluster and is a possible outlier.

5. Check the original seven values: the sum is 71, the centre is 9, and both extreme values give range 16.

**Answer:** mean ≈ 10.14; median = 9; range = 16; possible outlier = 21. **Misconception:** Keep the source, count, denominator and axis scale tied to the original dataset.
