## g9-data-l06-w01: Grouped travel times (minutes) have frequencies 0–9: 4, 10–19: 8, 20–29: 3. Find total frequency and the modal interval.

Visual: {"family": "data", "values": {"lesson": 6, "model": "histogram", "intervals": ["0–9", "10–19", "20–29"], "counts": [4, 8, 3], "total": 15, "modal_interval": "10–19"}}

1. Mark continuous time intervals 0–9, 10–19 and 20–29 on the horizontal axis.

2. Draw touching bars of heights 4, 8, 3 on a zero-based frequency axis.

3. Total frequency = 4 + 8 + 3 = 15.

4. The tallest bar has frequency 8, in interval 10–19 minutes.

5. Check: each journey contributes to one interval; the modal class is an interval, not an individual time.

**Answer:** total = 15; modal interval = 10–19 min. **Misconception:** Keep the source, count, denominator and axis scale tied to the original dataset.
