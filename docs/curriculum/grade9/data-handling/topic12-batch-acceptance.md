# Grade 9 Topic 12: Data Handling

[DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP, Term 3](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) covers data collection, representation, comparison, interpretation and reporting. Athena splits this into eight lessons with 120 worked examples, 120 four-choice practice items, 40 mastery items and 24 changed-value reassessments. Teacher review and desktop/mobile browser acceptance remain required.
