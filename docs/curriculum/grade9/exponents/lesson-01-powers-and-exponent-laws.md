### g9-exp-l01-p01
Question: Calculate 2⁴ × 2².
A. 64
B. 256
C. 20
D. 32
Key: A
Memo: 64. The bases match: both are 2. For multiplication add the exponents: 4+2=6. 2⁶ = 64. Check: 16 × 4 = 64.
Distractor 256: Incorrect: Multiplies exponents instead of adding them.
Distractor 20: Incorrect: Adds the separate power values.
Distractor 32: Incorrect: Omits one repeated base factor.
