### g9-exp-l02-p01
Question: Calculate (13)⁰.
A. 1
B. 0
C. 13
D. -1
Key: A
Memo: 1. The base 13 is nonzero. A nonzero base to exponent zero equals the multiplicative identity. Therefore (13)⁰ = 1. Check: 13³ ÷ 13³ = 1 = 13⁰.
Distractor 0: Incorrect: Treats a nonzero base raised to zero as zero.
Distractor 13: Incorrect: Keeps the base unchanged.
Distractor -1: Incorrect: Applies a negative sign without justification.
