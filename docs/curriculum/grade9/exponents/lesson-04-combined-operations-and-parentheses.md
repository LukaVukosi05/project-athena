### g9-exp-l04-w01 · foundation
Question: Calculate (2⁴ × 2³) ÷ 2².
Givens: 2; 4; 3; 2

2. Divide equal nonzero bases: subtract exponent 2, giving 7−2=5.
3. Evaluate 2⁵ = 32.
4. Check: 16×8÷4=32.
Answer: 32
