### g9-exp-l05-w01 · foundation
Question: A laboratory cuts identical fibres. Each unit measures 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ m. What is the total for 5 units in normalized scientific notation?
Givens: 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ m; 5
Visual: {"family": "context", "coefficient": "3.2", "exponent": -4, "multiplier": 5, "unit": "m", "result_coefficient": "1.6", "result_exponent": -3}

2. The unnormalized coefficient is 16.0.
3. Renormalize to 1.6 × 10⁻³ m.
4. Check: 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ m × 5 = 1.6 × 10⁻³ m.

### g9-exp-l05-p01
Question: A sensor records identical segments. Each unit measures 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ m. What is the total for 6 units in normalized scientific notation?
A. 7.2 × 10⁻⁴ m
B. 7.2 × 10⁻³ m
C. 7.2 × 10⁴ m
D. 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ m
Key: A
Memo: 7.2 × 10⁻⁴ m. Multiply 6 by the coefficient 1.2, keeping 10⁻⁴ and m. The unnormalized coefficient is 7.2. Renormalize to 7.2 × 10⁻⁴ m. Check: 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ m × 6 = 7.2 × 10⁻⁴ m.
Distractor 7.2 × 10⁻³ m: Incorrect: Loses a factor of ten when scaling the measured value.
Distractor 7.2 × 10⁴ m: Incorrect: Reverses the exponent direction.
Distractor 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ m: Incorrect: Omits the stated multiplier.
