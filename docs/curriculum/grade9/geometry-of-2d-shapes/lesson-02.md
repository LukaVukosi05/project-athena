## g9-shapes-l02-w01: In triangle ABC, ∠A = 22° and ∠B = 27°. Extend BC past C to D. Find exterior ∠ACD.

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "triangle", "lesson": 2, "left": 22, "right": 27, "target": 49, "model": "exterior"}}

1. Locate remote interior angles ∠A = 22° and ∠B = 27°.

2. The interior angle at C is 180° − 22° − 27° = 131°.

3. ∠ACB and ∠ACD form a straight line.

4. Calculate ∠ACD = 180° − 131° = 49°.

5. Check: the exterior angle also equals 22° + 27° = 49°.

**Answer:** ∠ACD = 49°. **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.
