## g9-shapes-l04-w01: A regular 5-sided polygon is drawn. Find its total interior angle sum and the size of each interior angle.

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "polygon", "lesson": 4, "model": "regular_polygon", "sides": 5, "target": 540, "each": "108"}}

1. Count 5 vertices; choose one vertex for diagonals.

2. Draw diagonals from that vertex, forming 3 non-overlapping triangles.

3. Each triangle contributes 180°: total = (5 − 2) × 180° = 540°.

4. Regularity makes all 5 interior angles equal: each = 540° ÷ 5 = 108°.

5. Check: 5 × 108° = 540°.

**Answer:** sum = 540°; each = 108°. **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.
