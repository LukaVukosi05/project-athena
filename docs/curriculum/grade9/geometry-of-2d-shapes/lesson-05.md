## g9-shapes-l05-w01: AB = DE = 5 cm, AC = DF = 8 cm, and BC = EF = 11 cm. Which condition proves △ABC ≅ △DEF?

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "pair", "lesson": 5, "model": "congruent", "sides": [5, 8, 11], "target": 11, "criterion": "SSS"}}

1. Mark AB = DE = 5 cm.

2. Mark AC = DF = 8 cm and BC = EF = 11 cm.

3. All three corresponding side pairs are equal.

4. Therefore △ABC ≅ △DEF by SSS.

5. Check the vertex order A↔D, B↔E, C↔F and all three side pairs.

**Answer:** △ABC ≅ △DEF (SSS). **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.

## g9-shapes-l05-w02: ∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E are marked equal. AB = DE = 6 cm lies between them. Which condition proves △ABC ≅ △DEF?

## g9-shapes-l05-w03: Triangles ABC and DEF have AB = DE = 7 cm, AC = DF = 5 cm and BC = 10 cm. Their included angles ∠A and ∠D are marked equal. Find EF and justify correspondence.

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "pair", "lesson": 5, "model": "congruent", "sides": [7, 5, 10], "target": 10, "criterion": "SAS"}}

1. Pair AB with DE (7 cm) and AC with DF (5 cm).

2. The included angles ∠A and ∠D are marked equal.

3. Two side pairs and their included angle establish SAS congruence: △ABC ≅ △DEF.

4. The order A↔D, B↔E, C↔F pairs BC with EF: EF = 10 cm.

5. Check: BC and EF are corresponding sides, both 10 cm.

**Answer:** EF = 10 cm. **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.
