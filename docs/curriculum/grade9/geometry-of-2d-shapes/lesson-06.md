## g9-shapes-l06-w01: △ABC ∼ △DEF in the stated order. AB = 4 cm, DE = 12 cm and BC = 6 cm. Find EF.

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "pair", "lesson": 6, "model": "similar", "small": 4, "scale": 3, "target": 18}}

1. Pair AB with DE and BC with EF in △ABC ∼ △DEF.

2. The scale factor from ABC to DEF is DE ÷ AB = 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

3. Write EF ÷ BC = 3, so EF = 3 × 6.

4. Calculate EF = 18 cm.

5. Check: EF ÷ BC = 18 ÷ 6 = 3.

**Answer:** EF = 18 cm. **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.
