## g9-shapes-l06-w01: ∠A = ∠D and ∠B = ∠E are marked equal. AB = 4 cm, DE = 12 cm and BC = 6 cm. Show the similarity condition and find EF.

Visual: {"family": "shapes", "values": {"kind": "pair", "lesson": 6, "model": "similar", "small": 4, "scale": 3, "target": 18, "criterion": "AA"}}

1. Locate the two corresponding equal angle pairs A↔D and B↔E.

2. Two equal angle pairs establish △ABC ∼ △DEF by AA.

3. Pair AB with DE: scale factor = 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

4. EF corresponds to BC, so EF = 3 × 6 = 18 cm.

5. Check: EF ÷ BC = 18 ÷ 6 = 3, and the two angle pairs match.

**Answer:** EF = 18 cm. **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.

## g9-shapes-l06-w03: AB = 6, AC = 7, BC = 8 cm; DE = 12, DF = 14, EF = 16 cm. Give the condition proving △ABC ∼ △DEF.

3. Pair BC:EF = 8:16 = 1:2.

4. All three corresponding side pairs are in the same ratio; △ABC ∼ △DEF by SSS similarity.

5. Check: every large side is 2 times its corresponding small side.

**Answer:** △ABC ∼ △DEF (SSS similarity). **Misconception:** A diagram alone does not prove a property; mark the given equality and state the applicable reason.
