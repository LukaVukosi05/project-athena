## g9-solid-l01-w01: A regular tetrahedron has 4 faces and 4 vertices; all its faces are triangles. Find the number of edges and verify using Euler’s relationship.

Visual: {"family": "solids", "values": {"lesson": 1, "model": "platonic", "shape": "tetrahedron", "faces": 4, "edges": 6, "vertices": 4, "face_shape": "triangles", "target": "edges"}}

1. Identify the tetrahedron; its faces are congruent regular triangles.

2. Label the givens: 4 faces and 4 vertices.

3. Use Euler’s relationship F + V = E + 2 with F = 4, E = 6, V = 4.

4. Therefore the missing number of edges is 6.

5. Check: 4 + 4 = 6 + 2 = 8.

**Answer:** edges = 6. **Misconception:** Count actual face pieces and their joining edges; keep curved surfaces distinct from flat faces.
