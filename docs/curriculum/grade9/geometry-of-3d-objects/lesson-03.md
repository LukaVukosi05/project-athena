Authority: [DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf), Term 4. Original Athena questions.

Status: teacher and browser review required. Each operation updates the net/solid and a cumulative written solution.

## g9-solid-l03-w01: A cube is built from a net with one labelled edge of 2 cm. Name every face piece needed for the complete net before folding.

Visual: {"family": "solids", "values": {"lesson": 3, "model": "net", "shape": "cube", "faces": 6, "scale": 2, "parts": "6 equal squares", "missing": false}}

1. Identify the solid as a cube.

2. Find the base pieces; match each shared edge to the corresponding side.

3. Arrange side pieces beside their matching base edges. The net needs 6 equal squares.

4. Count the complete net: 6 face pieces; fold adjacent matching edges.

5. Check that the folded net closes with 6 faces and no gap or overlap.

**Answer:** cube net: 6 equal squares. **Misconception:** Count actual face pieces and their joining edges; keep curved surfaces distinct from flat faces.
