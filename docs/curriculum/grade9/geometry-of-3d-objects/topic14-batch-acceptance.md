# Grade 9 Topic 14: Geometry of 3D Objects

[DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP, Term 4](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) covers Platonic solids, spheres, cylinders and nets of cubes, prisms, pyramids and cylinders. Four Athena lessons contain 60 worked examples, 60 practice items, 20 mastery items and 12 reassessments. Teacher and browser acceptance remain required.
