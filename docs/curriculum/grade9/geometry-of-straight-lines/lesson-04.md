## g9-lines-l04-w01: Two marked parallel lines have an upper top-right angle of 47°. The lower top-left angle is labelled (3x + 124)°. Find x, then calculate the marked angle.

Visual: {"family": "straight_lines", "values": {"kind": "parallel", "theta": 47, "lesson": 4, "relation": "algebra", "given": 47, "target": 133, "coefficient": 3, "constant": 124, "solution": 3, "middle": 47}}

1. Mark the given upper top-right sector as 47°.

2. Its lower top-right corresponding partner is also 47°.

3. The lower top-left sector and that partner lie on a straight line, so its measure is 180° − 47° = 133°.

4. Equate its label to its measure: 3x + 124 = 133.

5. Subtract 124 from both sides: 3x = 9.

6. Divide both sides by 3: x = 3; the marked angle is 133°.

7. Check: 3(3) + 124 = 133, and 133° + 47° = 180°.

**Answer:** x = 3. **Misconception:** Name the exact angle pair and its parallel or perpendicular markings; visual appearance alone is not proof.
