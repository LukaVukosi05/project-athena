# Grade 9 Topic 9: Geometry of Straight Lines

[DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP, Term 2](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) requires angle relationships formed by perpendicular, intersecting and parallel lines, then problems with reasons. Athena's five-lesson breakdown is a product decision.

Every lesson has 15 worked examples, 15 four-choice practice items, five mastery and three changed-value reassessments. Teacher mathematical and accessibility review plus real desktop/mobile browser acceptance remain required.
