# Grade 9 Topic 8: Graphs

[DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP, Term 2](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) covers global features, linear gradient and intercepts, ordered pairs, drawing from equations and equations from graphs. Seven lessons are Athena's product breakdown.

Each lesson includes 15 original worked examples, 15 separate four-option practice questions, five mastery and three changed-value reassessments. The renderer should plot each question's points incrementally on a persistent coordinate plane and keep a cumulative written record.

Teacher review and real desktop/mobile browser acceptance remain required before release. Structural and arithmetic tests do not establish learning outcomes.
