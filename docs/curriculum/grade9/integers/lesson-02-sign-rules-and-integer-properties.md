### g9-int-l02-w01 · foundation
Question: Evaluate (-6) + 9 + (-9).
Givens: -6; 9; -9
Visual: {"family": "tiles", "values": [-6, 9, -9], "operator": "inverse", "path": [0, -6]}

2. Add the inverses: 9 + (-9) = 0.
3. Adding zero leaves -6.
4. Check: Substituting the original signed values also gives -6.
Answer: -6

### g9-int-l02-w07 · standard
Question: Evaluate ((-12) + 9) + (-4) by regrouping.
Givens: -12; 9; -4
Visual: {"family": "tiles", "values": [-12, 9, -4], "operator": "associative", "path": [0, -7]}
1. Keep every signed term: -12, 9, -4.

Answer: -7

## Practice · private key and rationale

### g9-int-l02-p01
Question: Expand and evaluate (-5) × (8 + (-3)).
A. -25
B. -43
C. -55
D. 0
Key: A
Memo: -25. The bracket contains two terms: 8 and -3. Distribute -5 to both: -5×8 + -5×(-3) = -40 + 15. Add the signed products: -25. Check: Substituting the original signed values also gives -25.
Distractor -43: Incorrect: Multiplies only the first bracket term.
Distractor -55: Incorrect: Subtracts the second term after distribution.
Distractor 0: Incorrect: Adds the three original values without multiplying.
