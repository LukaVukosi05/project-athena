### g9-int-l03-p01
Question: Calculate ∛(-64).
A. -4
B. 4
C. -12
D. -64
Key: A
Memo: -4. A cube root is the number repeated as a product of three equal factors. -4×-4×-4 = -64. Therefore ∛(-64) = -4. Check: -4³ = -64.
Distractor 4: Incorrect: Reverses the sign of the cube root.
Distractor -12: Incorrect: Multiplies the root by three.
Distractor -64: Incorrect: Returns the original cube instead of its root.
