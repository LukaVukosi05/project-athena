## Practice · private key and rationale

### g9-int-l04-p01
Question: Calculate ((-6) + 4) × (-2).
A. 4
B. -14
C. 20
D. -4
Key: A
Memo: 4. Evaluate the bracket first: -6 + 4 = -2. The expression becomes (-2)×(-2). Apply the sign rule and multiply magnitudes: 4. Check: Replaying both changes from -6 gives 4.
Distractor -14: Incorrect: Multiplies before completing the bracket.
Distractor 20: Incorrect: Treats the plus sign in the bracket as a minus.
Distractor -4: Incorrect: Reverses the sign of a correct product.

A. -1°C
B. -7°C
C. -17°C
D. -4°C
Key: B

Distractor -1°C: Incorrect: Moves up for a fall in temperature.
Distractor -17°C: Incorrect: Moves down for the stated positive change.
Distractor -4°C: Incorrect: Stops before the final fall.
