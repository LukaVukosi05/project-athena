## g9-prob-l02-w01: A survey records Grade 9 bus 6, Grade 9 walk 4, Grade 8 bus 5, Grade 8 walk 8. Find P(bus | Grade 9).

Visual: {"family": "probability", "values": {"lesson": 2, "model": "two_way", "table": [[6, 4], [5, 8]], "row_total": 10, "column_total": 11, "grand_total": 23}}

1. Write the table: Grade 9 bus 6, walk 4; Grade 8 bus 5, walk 8.

2. Grade 9 row total = 6 + 4 = 10; the full survey has 23.

3. Given Grade 9, restrict attention to the 10 Grade 9 learners.

4. Of these, 6 use the bus: P(bus | Grade 9) = 6/10 = 3/5.

5. Check the condition: use the Grade 9 row denominator 10, not the grand total 23.

**Answer:** P(bus | Grade 9) = 3/5. **Misconception:** Choose the correct sample space and denominator, then distinguish a path product from addition and observation from prediction.

Options: P(bus | Grade 9) = 44/49 | P(bus | Grade 9) = 45/49 | P(bus | Grade 9) = 6/7 | P(bus | Grade 9) = 43/49. Correct: P(bus | Grade 9) = 43/49. Write the table: Grade 9 bus 43, walk 6; Grade 8 bus 6, walk 9. Grade 9 row total = 43 + 6 = 49; the full survey has 64. Given Grade 9, restrict attention to the 49 Grade 9 learners. Of these, 43 use the bus: P(bus | Grade 9) = 43/49. Check the condition: use the Grade 9 row denominator 49, not the grand total 64.
