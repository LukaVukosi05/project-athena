## g9-prob-l03-w01: A bag has 4 red and 3 blue counters. Draw twice without replacement. Find P(red then red).

Visual: {"family": "probability", "values": {"lesson": 3, "model": "tree", "red": 4, "blue": 3, "first_red_num": 4, "first_red_den": 7, "second_red_num": 3, "second_red_den": 6, "prob_num": 2, "prob_den": 7}}

1. First draw: 4 red out of 7 counters, so P(R₁) = 4/7.

2. After a red draw, 3 red counters remain among 6.

3. Follow the red-to-red path: P(R₂ | R₁) = 3/6.

4. Multiply along that path: (4/7) × (3/6) = 2/7.

5. Check the second denominator decreased by one because the first counter was not replaced.

**Answer:** P(red then red) = 2/7. **Misconception:** Choose the correct sample space and denominator, then distinguish a path product from addition and observation from prediction.
