# Grade 9 Topic 13 · Lesson 4: Experimental versus theoretical relative frequency

Status: teacher and browser review required. Show the sample space, denominators, branches or trials before the result.

## g9-prob-l04-w01: A fair coin gave heads 15 times in 22 tosses. Find the experimental relative frequency of heads and the theoretical prediction for heads in 50 new tosses.

Visual: {"family": "probability", "values": {"lesson": 4, "model": "relative_frequency", "trials": 22, "heads": 15, "tails": 7, "predicted_heads": 25}}

1. Observed heads = 15; observed tails = 7; total = 22.

2. Experimental relative frequency of heads is 15/22.

3. The fair-coin theoretical probability of heads is 1/2, which need not equal 15/22.

4. Theoretical prediction for 50 new tosses = 50 × 1/2 = 25.

5. Check: observed 15/22 describes past trials; 25 is an expectation, not a guarantee.

**Answer:** experimental frequency = 15/22; predicted heads in 50 = 25. **Misconception:** Choose the correct sample space and denominator, then distinguish a path product from addition and observation from prediction.
