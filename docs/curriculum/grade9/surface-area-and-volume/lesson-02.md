## g9-sv-l02-w01: A water tank is a rectangular prism with internal length 6 cm, width 5 cm and height 4 cm. Find its volume in cm³ and capacity in litres.

Visual: {"family": "surface_volume", "values": {"lesson": 2, "model": "box_layers", "length": 6, "width": 5, "height": 4, "volume": 120, "capacity_num": 3, "capacity_den": 25}}

1. Mark the internal dimensions 6 cm by 5 cm by 4 cm.

2. One base layer covers 6 × 5 = 30 cm².

3. Stack 4 such 1 cm layers: 30 × 4 = 120 cm³.

4. Use 1 000 cm³ = 1 L: capacity = 120/1000 = 3/25 L.

5. Check: 3/25 × 1000 = 120 cm³; the units agree.

**Answer:** volume = 120 cm³; capacity = 3/25 L. **Misconception:** Distinguish length, square and cubic units; count each face once, then verify the given radius, height and conversion.
