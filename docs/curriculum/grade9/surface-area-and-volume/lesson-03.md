## g9-sv-l03-w01: A right triangular prism has perpendicular triangle sides 6 cm and 8 cm, third side 10 cm, and prism length 5 cm. Find its volume and total surface area.

Visual: {"family": "surface_volume", "values": {"lesson": 3, "model": "tri_prism", "base": 6, "altitude": 8, "hypotenuse": 10, "length": 5, "triangle_area": 24, "volume": 120, "surface_area": 168}}

1. Confirm the triangle is right-angled: 6² + 8² = 10².

2. One triangle has area ½ × 6 × 8 = 24 cm².

3. Volume = triangular base area × length = 24 × 5 = 120 cm³.

4. Total surface area = 2(24) + (6 + 8 + 10) × 5 = 168 cm².

5. Check: two triangular ends and three rectangles are counted; cm² differs from cm³.

**Answer:** volume = 120 cm³; surface area = 168 cm². **Misconception:** Distinguish length, square and cubic units; count each face once, then verify the given radius, height and conversion.
