# Grade 9 Topic 15: Surface Area and Volume

[DBE 2026 Grade 9 Mathematics ATP, Term 4](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%209.pdf) includes cubes, rectangular and triangular prisms, cylinders, SI capacity conversions and volume changes when dimensions double. Six Athena lessons contain 90 worked examples, 90 four-choice practice items, 30 mastery items and 18 reassessments. Teacher and browser acceptance remain required.
