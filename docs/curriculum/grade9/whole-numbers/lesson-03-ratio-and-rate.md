## Worked examples

### g9-whole-l03-w01 · foundation
Question: A box has 18 blue and 30 red counters. Write the blue:red ratio in simplest form.
Givens: Blue 18; Red 30
Visual: ratio · {"family": "ratio", "bars": [18, 30], "labels": ["blue", "red"]}
1. Identify the ordered ratio blue:red as 18:30.
2. The greatest common factor of 18 and 30 is 6.
3. Divide both parts by 6: 18÷6:30÷6 = 3:5.
4. Check: 3×6=18 and 5×6=30; the order remains blue:red.
Answer: 3:5
Misconception: ratio-order-or-one-sided-scaling

### g9-whole-l03-w11 · exam
Question: Pack A costs R48 for 2 kg; pack B costs R90 for 3 kg. Which costs less per kg, and by how much?

Visual: rate · {"family": "rate", "table": [[48, 2], [90, 3]], "units": ["R", "kg"]}
