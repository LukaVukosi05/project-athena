## Worked examples

### g9-whole-l05-w01 · foundation
Question: A jacket marked R800 is discounted by 10%. What is its sale price?
Givens: R800; 10% discount
Visual: transaction · {"family": "transaction", "timeline": [800, 80, 720], "labels": ["marked", "discount", "sale"]}
1. The percentage base is the marked price, R800.
2. The discount is 10% of R800 = R80.
3. Subtract the discount: R800−R80=R720.
4. Check: R720+R80=R800.
Answer: R720
Misconception: wrong-percentage-base

### g9-whole-l05-w11 · exam
Question: A device priced R2 000 gets 20% off, then 15% VAT on the reduced price. What is the final price?

Visual: transaction · {"family": "transaction", "timeline": [2000, 1600, 1840], "labels": ["original", "after discount", "after VAT"]}

Answer: R1 840
Misconception: sequential-base

A. R810
B. R90
C. R990
D. R890

Distractor R90: Incorrect: Reports the discount or VAT component instead of final price.
