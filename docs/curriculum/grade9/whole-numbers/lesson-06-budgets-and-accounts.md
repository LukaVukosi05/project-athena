## Worked examples

### g9-whole-l06-w01 · foundation
Question: A household receives R8 000 monthly. Rent, food and transport are R3 000, R1 800 and R900; other costs are R300. What remains?
Givens: Income R8 000; R3 000; R1 800; R900; Other R300
Visual: ledger · {"family": "ledger", "income": 8000, "expenses": [3000, 1800, 900, 300], "balance": 2000}
1. Use a monthly ledger; start with income R8 000.
2. Add expenses: R3 000 + R1 800 + R900 + R300 = R6 000.
3. Subtract total spending from income: R8 000−R6 000=R2 000.
4. Check: R2 000+R6 000=R8 000.
Answer: R2 000
Misconception: debit-credit

### g9-whole-l06-w06 · standard

Visual: ledger · {"family": "ledger", "income": 9000, "expenses": [3000, 2100, 1200], "balance": 2700, "plan": 800}
1. The expense base is the same month as income.
2. After fixed expenses, R9 000−R6 300=R2 700 remains.
3. Compare R2 700 with R800: affordable.
4. Check: R2 700 ≥ R800.
Answer: Affordable
Misconception: budget-time-base

### g9-whole-l06-p01
Question: A household receives R9 500 monthly. Rent, food and transport are R3 200, R2 200 and R1 100; other costs are R500. What remains?
A. R2 500
B. R7 000
C. R16 500
D. R6 300
Key: A
Memo: R2 500. Use a monthly ledger; start with income R9 500. Add expenses: R3 200 + R2 200 + R1 100 + R500 = R7 000. Subtract total spending from income: R9 500−R7 000=R2 500. Check: R2 500+R7 000=R9 500.
Distractor R7 000: Incorrect: Reports total expenses as balance.
Distractor R16 500: Incorrect: Adds expenses to income.
Distractor R6 300: Incorrect: Forgets other expenses.
