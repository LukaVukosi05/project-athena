## Worked examples

### g9-whole-l07-w01 · foundation
Question: Invest R2 000 at 5% simple interest per year for 2 years. Find the final balance.
Givens: R2 000; 5% p.a.; 2 years
Visual: interest · {"family": "interest", "balances": [2000, 2100, 2200], "kind": "simple"}
1. With simple interest, every year uses the original R2 000 as base.
2. One year adds 5% of R2 000 = R100; 2 years add R200.
3. Final balance is R2 000+R200=R2 200.
4. Check: R2 200−R2 000=R200=2×R100.
Answer: R2 200
Misconception: compound-instead-of-simple

### g9-whole-l07-p01
Question: Invest R2 500 at 4% simple interest per year for 2 years. Find the final balance.
A. R2 700
B. R200
C. R2 600
D. R2 800
Key: A
Memo: R2 700. With simple interest, every year uses the original R2 500 as base. One year adds 4% of R2 500 = R100; 2 years add R200. Final balance is R2 500+R200=R2 700. Check: R2 700−R2 500=R200=2×R100.
Distractor R200: Incorrect: Reports interest without the principal.
Distractor R2 600: Incorrect: Counts only one year.
Distractor R2 800: Incorrect: Counts an extra year.
