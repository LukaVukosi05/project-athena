## Worked examples

### g9-whole-l08-w01 · foundation
Question: At R20 per foreign unit, exchange R1 800. The provider then charges 5 foreign units. How many foreign units remain?
Givens: R1 800; R20/unit; 5 units fee
Visual: exchange · {"family": "exchange", "source": 1800, "rate": 20, "gross": 90, "fee": 5, "net": 85}
1. Check units: rand ÷ (rand per foreign unit) gives foreign units.
2. Convert: R1 800÷R20 = 90 foreign units.
3. Deduct the foreign-currency fee: 90−5=85 foreign units.
4. Check: 85+5=90; 90×20=1800 rand.
Answer: 85 foreign units
Misconception: exchange-direction
