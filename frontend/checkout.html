A little clarity before you begin.

When does my trial start?

After your payment method is successfully authorised. Creating an account does not start the clock. Your confirmation will show the exact date your 14-day trial ends.

When will I be charged?

The offer is R0 for 14 full days, then R149 per month until you cancel. You will see the first billing date before confirming a live payment agreement. Payment setup currently offers an access preview only, without a real payment agreement or charges.

How do I cancel?

Go to Account → Manage trial and billing. Cancel before the first charge to pay nothing. Your access remains available until the current trial or paid period ends.

Is this suitable for my child?

Athena covers Grade 8–12 CAPS maths, with lesson explanations and independent practice. A parent or guardian must manage the payment agreement for a learner under 18. Explore a lesson to decide whether the teaching suits your learner.

Where do I enter payment details?

Card entry will take place with the payment provider when payments are connected. Athena does not ask for card numbers or security codes in this access preview. Reminder emails are not sent during the preview.
