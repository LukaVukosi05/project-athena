Simple trial details

Your 14-day period starts when payment-method authorisation succeeds, not when you create an account or open checkout. The first subscription charge is due only after those 14 full days. The confirmation shows the exact date and time.

After the trial, the plan renews monthly at R149 until you cancel. Cancel through Account → Manage trial and billing. Canceling a trial before its first charge prevents that charge; access lasts until the end of the trial. One trial per account.

For learners under 18, a parent or guardian must manage the payment agreement. In live checkout, card details will be entered on the payment provider’s secure page.

This build is a simulation. No real payment authorisation, email delivery or automatic collection takes place. Trial access dates and simulated actions are saved for testing; local curriculum testing stays unlocked through the development launcher.
